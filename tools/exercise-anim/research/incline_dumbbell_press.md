# Incline Dumbbell Press — research targets vs the clip (v4, 15 Oct 2026)

Sources: Rodríguez-Ridao 2020 (bench angle EMG, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7579505/), Lauver 2016
(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25799093/), Coratella 2020, ACE (https://www.acefitness.org/exercise-library-details/8/25/),
NASM. No study measured the dumbbell version's joint angles: angles below combine coaching cues with the barbell studies.

| What | Research target | Clip v3 (before) | Clip v4 (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bench angle | 30° (upper-chest peak; >45° shifts to front delt) | 35° | 30° (torso 30.8°) |
| Elbow flare at the bottom (upper arm from torso) | 45–60° | 77° | 59° |
| Elbow bend at the bottom | ~90° | 151° | 94° |
| Forearm | vertical through the rep | 21–46° tilt | 1–6° |
| Depth | handles level with the upper chest | — | upper arm just below the torso line |
| Top | arms straight but soft, dumbbells over eyes/upper chest, nearly touching | 74° bent | ~7° soft, handles 0.42 m apart |
| Path | vertical to the floor, slight inward arc | — | vertical (side view) |
| Tempo | ~2 s up, ~2 s down | 1.1 / 1.4 s | 1.6 s up, 0.4 hold, 2.0 down, 0.3 pause |

Muscles (EMG, barbell incline): upper (clavicular) chest ~30 % MVIC and front delt ~33 % at 30° → both primary;
triceps ~15 % → secondary; lower (sternal) chest lower than flat → dim; side delt ~5 % → off.
Clip highlight: chest_upper 1.0, front_delts 1.0, triceps 0.55, chest_lower 0.45.
Surface: clavicular head = under the inner half of the collarbone (the outer half is front-delt origin);
sternal head = lower two-thirds of the fan, lower border rising toward the armpit; front delt = front of the shoulder
cap to ~40 % down the arm; triceps = back of the upper arm.

Common mistakes avoided: bench too steep, elbows flared to 90°, tilted forearms, short range, wrong muscles lit.
