# Lateral Raise — research targets vs the clip (v4, 15 Oct 2026)

Sources: Campos 2020 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7706677), Coratella 2020
(https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7503819/), Reinold 2007, Ludewig 2009 (scapulohumeral rhythm),
ACE (https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/26/lateral-raise/).

| What | Research target | Clip v3 (before) | Clip v4 (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top height (arm from the side) | 90° | 102° | 88° |
| Plane | 15–30° in front of side-on (scapular plane) | 30° behind the body | 20–25° in front |
| Elbow | soft 15–20°, held the same all rep | 0° → 81° | 14–17° |
| Hand vs elbow at the top | at or just below the elbow | below | 3 cm below |
| Rotation | palms in → palms down, no thumb-down "pour" | — | palms down, front edge slightly up |
| Shoulder blade | rotates up ~30° at the top, no shrug | fixed small lift | small clavicle lift at the top only |
| Trunk | still, upright | upright | upright |
| Tempo | ~2 s up, 0.5 s hold, 2 s down | 1.05 / 1.35 s | 1.6 up, 0.4 hold, 1.9 down, 0.3 pause |

Muscles (EMG %MVIC, Campos 2020): side delt 30 %, rear delt 24 %, front delt 21 %; upper traps rise with shrugging or
the thumb-down turn; supraspinatus works but is deep (not shown); serratus minor below 120°.
Clip highlight: side_delts 1.0, traps 0.5, front_delts 0.4, rear_delts 0.4. App library (side_delts primary, traps
secondary) is right; front/rear delt added in the clip only.
Surface: side delt = outer shoulder cap, acromion to ~40 % down the arm; upper traps = neck to shoulder-tip slope.
