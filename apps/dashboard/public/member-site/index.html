Watch app

Log every set from your wrist.

The free MettleUp watch app works on Android (Wear OS) watches. Your phone stays in your pocket.

  1. See your next set.The weight, the reps and last time's numbers.
  2. Turn the dial.The weight changes in 2.5 kg steps.
  3. Tap the tick.The set is saved on your phone, and your rest starts.
  4. Your watch tells you when rest is over.Your heart rate stays on the screen too.

Records

Catch every new record.

Lift your heaviest weight or most reps ever, and a pop-up names the record the moment it happens.

When you finish, see today's total kg next to last time.

Progress

See your strength grow.

Every exercise has a graph of your heaviest weight. A body picture shows which muscles you trained in the last 7 days.

Plans

Pick a plan, or make your own.

Choose a ready-made plan. Or the app builds one from your goal, your training days, your equipment and any sore joints.

  • Are you moving from Hevy or Strong? Import your old workouts and keep your history.
  • 402 exercises, warm-up sets, drop sets and supersets.
  • The rest timer starts when you tick a set. It rings even with your phone locked.
  • A 1-rep max and % calculator on every exercise page.
  • Progress photos stay on your phone, so they stay private.
  • Back up to your own Google Drive, and get it all back on a new phone.
