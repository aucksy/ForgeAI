Catch every new record.

Lift your heaviest weight or most reps ever, and a pop-up names the record the moment it happens.

When you finish a workout, see the total kg you lifted next to last time.

A rest timer that rings on time.

Tick a set and your rest starts by itself. It rings when rest is over, even with your phone locked.

Choose the sound: chime, bell, double beep or whistle.

Coming soon The watch app: your rest countdown, next set and heart rate on your Wear OS watch.

See your strength grow.

Every exercise has a graph of your heaviest weight: 3 months, 12 months or all time.

A body picture shows which muscles you trained in the last 7 days.

Pick a plan, or make your own.

Choose a ready-made plan, or let the app build one from your goal, training days, equipment and sore joints. Or make your own routines.

Also in the app

  • Moving from Hevy or Strong? Import your old workouts and keep your history.
  • 402 exercises, many with pictures of the start and end position, so you can check your form.
  • Warm-up sets, drop sets and supersets.
  • A 1-rep max and % calculator on every exercise page.
  • Progress photos that stay on your phone, so they stay private.
  • A big “In use” screen while you rest, so others know the machine is taken.
  • Back up to your own Google Drive and get it all back on a new phone.

Get the app, free.

  1. Tap “Download the app”.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If your phone asks, allow installs from Chrome.
  3. Open MettleUp, type your name and start your first workout.
