MettleUp data agreement for gyms

Version 2026-10-24. Between MettleUp, run by Aakash Pahuja, Jaipur, India ("we"), and the gym whose owner ticks "I agree" ("you"). It is part of the MettleUp terms.

1. Your records are yours. Your members' details, memberships, payments, receipts, check-ins, shop orders, staff and messages are your gym's records. Under India's data law (DPDP Act 2023) you are the Data Fiduciary for them and we are your Data Processor.

2. What we do with them. We store and process them only to run MettleUp for your gym, and only as the app's features and your staff's actions direct. We do not sell them, show ads with them, use them for our own marketing, or share them with other gyms. We may count them in totals that name no gym or person (for example, how many check-ins MettleUp handled).

3. Who else handles them (our service providers).

  • Supabase: our database, in Mumbai, India.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the admin app and passes requests to the database; it also keeps our feedback box and daily AI-use counts.
  • Google Gemini, then Anthropic Claude if Gemini fails: only when your owner imports a member list. They see the column names, the first 5 rows of the file and simple counts per column. Names, addresses, mobile numbers, emails, PAN and ID numbers, and any run of 7 or more digits are hidden first, twice (in your browser and again on our server): a column of names or addresses shows only "[name]" or "[address]". Plan names, amounts and dates in those 5 rows are not hidden. The AI only answers which column is which; we keep nothing from the file. These companies are outside India.

We tell you before we add a new provider that sees your records.

4. Security. Data travels encrypted. Each gym can reach only its own records. Staff sign in with single-use codes, and only people the owner adds can sign in. Every receipt records who took the money. The admin app keeps a copy of your records on each device a staff member signs in on, so the desk works without internet; signing that device out deletes the copy.

5. If something leaks. We tell you in writing within 24 hours of finding out: what happened, what data, what we did, and what comes next. We help you send the Data Protection Board your first notice straight away and the full report within 72 hours, and tell the members affected.

6. Your duties.

  • Tell your members that you keep their records in MettleUp (a short notice you can copy is in our privacy notice), and have a lawful reason or their consent to keep them.
  • Keep the records accurate, and correct them when a member asks.
  • Add only people who need access, and remove staff who leave (Settings → people).
  • Use members' details only for your gym's work.
  • Tell us at once if a phone or computer with the app is lost, or data looks exposed.
  • Only import a member list you are allowed to use.

7. Members' own requests. If a member asks you to see, correct or erase their details, you handle it. We help when you ask. If a member asks us, we pass it to you.

8. Getting your data out. At any time, the owner can download everything: Settings → Your data → Download all your gym's data (an Excel file and a full copy). If that does not work for you, we send you the same within 7 days of asking.

9. Deleting your gym. The owner asks in Settings → Your data → Delete our gym from MettleUp, or by email. We call the gym's own number to confirm, remind you to download first, then delete every member, payment, check-in and shop order of your gym from our server within 30 days of the request. Backup copies expire within 7 more days. We keep the request itself, and the record that you accepted this agreement, for 2 years, as proof.

10. Members' own saved workouts are not your records. A member who signs in with Google can save their own workouts, body weight and measurements on MettleUp. That copy belongs to the member; MettleUp is responsible for it, not your gym. You, your trainers and your desk cannot see it, and deleting your gym does not delete it.

11. Our record of your acceptance. When your owner ticks "I agree", we keep: the gym, who ticked it (their name as signed in), when, which version, whether it was at "Create my gym" or at a later sign-in, and a one-way scrambled form of the internet address it came from. That scrambled form can still be traced back, so we protect it like any other personal data.

12. Term and ending. This agreement runs while your gym uses MettleUp. You can stop at any time and ask us to delete your gym (point 9). We may end it with 30 days' written notice, or at once for serious misuse; you can still download your data during the notice. Points 5, 9 and 10 continue after it ends, until your records are deleted.

13. Liability. MettleUp is a beta.

14. Changes. We tell the owner in the admin app before a new version applies, and ask the owner to accept it again. The version date is at the top.

15. Law. Indian law; courts in Jaipur. Contact: Aakash Pahuja, contact@mettleup.fit.
