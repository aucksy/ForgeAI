Our main database is in Mumbai, India (Supabase). Some service providers may process limited data outside India: Cloudflare (delivers the desk app), Google Play and GitHub (app downloads) [and Sentry (crash reports), once added]. They process it only to provide their service to us.

Your rights

You can:

  • ask for a summary of the data we hold about you and who we shared it with;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to erase it, where the law allows (your gym may have to keep payment records);
  • withdraw consent: tap Leave gym in the app, or email [CONTACT EMAIL]. It is as easy as giving it. It stops future use only; some features then stop working;
  • name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer above. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Under 18

The member app is only for people aged 18 or over. If we learn we hold data of someone under 18 without a parent's consent, we delete it.

Security and breaches

Data travels encrypted; each gym can only reach its own records; staff sign in with single-use codes; every receipt records who took the money. If your data is exposed, we tell your gym within 24 hours and report it to the Data Protection Board as the law requires, and you are told directly (in the app or by message) what happened, what it means and what to do.
