1. Who we are

MettleUp is a workout app for gym members, on Android phones and Wear OS watches. Gyms also use a MettleUp web app, the admin app, to run their gym. In this notice, "we" means MettleUp. MettleUp is run by Aakash Pahuja, a sole proprietor in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.

This notice covers the MettleUp app, the watch app, the admin app, and our websites mettleup.fit, gyms.mettleup.fit and app.mettleup.fit.

Questions and complaints: our Grievance Officer is Aakash Pahuja, contact@mettleup.fit. We answer within 30 days.

2. Who is in charge of your data

  • MettleUp is in charge of your MettleUp account and the copy of your workouts saved with it. We are also in charge of help and feedback messages, crash reports, workouts you share with gym mates, and our technical logs. (Under India's data law, we are the "Data Fiduciary" for these.)
  • Your gym is in charge of its own records about you. These are your membership, payments, receipts, check-ins, shop orders and messages. They also include your photo taken at the desk, measurements and diet plans from your trainer, and your personal training bookings. The things the app sends your gym (section 4B) are the gym's records too. We only store these records for your gym, under a written agreement. (Your gym is the "Data Fiduciary" and we are its "Data Processor".) Ask your gym first about these records. Its name is in the app under Settings → Your gym. If you write to us instead, we pass your request to your gym and help it answer.

3. What stays on your phone

Everything you log stays on your phone unless this notice says otherwise. That means your workouts, routines, notes, body weight, measurements and profile. Your progress photos never leave your phone.

"Download my data" (Settings → Your data → Backup and restore) makes one file of your data. The file stays on your phone until you choose where to keep it. "Ask an AI about your workouts" (Settings → Your data) only opens your phone's share menu. You pick the app, and MettleUp itself sends nothing.

4. What we collect, and why

A. If you sign in with Google

Signing in is your choice. You sign in with "Continue with Google", on the welcome screen or in Settings → Your data → Backup and restore.

  • From Google: your email address. If Google shares them, also your name and a link to your profile picture.
  • A copy of what you log:
    • your workouts: every exercise and set, with weights, reps, times, distances, effort ratings and notes;
    • your routines, the exercises you made yourself, and your settings for rest timers and personal records;
    • the exercises of your last "empty workout", so the next one starts with them;
    • your body weight and measurements, including weigh-ins brought in from Health Connect;
    • the dates of your progress photos, but never the photos;
    • your profile: name, age, height, goal, experience, gym name, member-since date, food targets, units, language, bio and link.
    This copy never holds your mobile number.
  • Why: so your workouts come back on a new phone, or after you reinstall the app.
  • Who can see it: only you. Your gym and other members cannot. We look at it only to fix a fault, or when the law makes us.

  • Find my gym: if you check your mobile number in the app, we look for it on the member lists of gyms that use MettleUp, only to show you your own gym. The number is checked once and is not kept for this.
  • Tell my gym: if you type your gym's name and city, we keep only those two, so our team can contact that gym. We never share your number.

C. Health Connect and your watch

In Settings → Phone and watch → "Health apps", you choose what MettleUp may do. Health Connect asks for your permission first, each time:

  • Save workouts: each finished workout (the exercise, its start and end, and active calories) goes to Health Connect. Google Health, Samsung Health and other apps can then show it.
  • Body weight: weigh-ins from a smart scale or another app are added to your body-weight log. Like the rest of that log, they are in your MettleUp copy if you signed in.
  • Steps: today's steps are shown on that page and are not stored.
  • "Sleep and workouts": the app reads last night's sleep (its start, end and stages) on your phone. After a lighter workout, it tells you if you slept under 6 hours. Sleep data is not stored, not backed up and not sent anywhere.

Data from Health Connect never goes to your gym, to advertisers or to any AI. MettleUp's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. To stop, tap "Stop and remove MettleUp's access" on that page, or change it in Health Connect.

Your watch: the phone and watch apps send your workout to each other directly, through Google Play services on your own devices. During a workout, the watch shows your heart rate from its own sensor. Your heart rate is not saved or sent. The phone reads the watch app's version, so it can tell you when an update is ready.

D. Help, feedback and crash reports

  • Help and feedback (Settings → Help and feedback): we get your message, the app and its version, your phone model, the screen you were on and your gym's code. We also get a random number for this install and a coded form of your internet (IP) address, used only to stop misuse. We do not get your name or mobile number, unless you type them.
  • Help questions: to write an instant answer, Anthropic's Claude AI reads your message, the app, the screen and the app version. Anthropic does not use it to train its AI. Our own reply is saved with your question.
  • Spoken feedback (the "Speak" button): your recording goes through our server to Groq, which turns it into text. Neither we nor Groq keep the recording. To stop misuse, we count how many recordings each phone and each coded internet address makes a day.
  • "Add my watch's screen log" (off unless you switch it on): the times your watch screen turned on, off or dim, and your watch's Battery Saver and screen settings.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes, it sends a short error message, where in the app it broke, the screen and the app version. Anything that looks like an email, phone number or long number is removed first. A report never holds your workouts, name, gym or phone details. The error text is copied to our private bug list on GitHub.

E. Gym owners and staff (the admin app)

  • Your name, mobile number and role in the gym. Your PIN is kept only in a coded form that cannot be turned back into the PIN.
  • Sign-in records: who signed in, on which device, from which internet address, and when. Wrong attempts are recorded too.
  • WhatsApp checks: to start a gym or reset a PIN, you may send a WhatsApp message with a code to MettleUp's WhatsApp number. WhatsApp (Meta) tells us which number sent it. We check that it is your number, and keep only a coded form of it.
  • Your acceptance of our terms: who accepted, when, which version, and a coded form of the internet address. Someone could still match that code to an address, so we protect it like other personal data.
  • The internet address a gym was created from, to stop misuse.
  • Payments to MettleUp: the plan, the amount, the date, and the UPI or bank reference number you send.
  • The gym's own records (members, plans, payments, check-ins, shop, staff, messages and photos) belong to the gym (section 2). So the desk works without internet, the admin app keeps a copy on each device a staff member signs in on. Signing that device out deletes the copy.
  • Importing an old member list: to work out which column is which, Google Gemini (or Anthropic's Claude if Gemini fails) sees the column names, 5 example rows and simple counts. Names, addresses, mobile numbers, emails and ID numbers are hidden before they are sent. Plan names, amounts and dates are not hidden. The AI sends back no member details.
  • MettleUp support looks at a gym's records only in a short window. Usually the gym's owner opens it, for 1 hour. In an emergency, such as a fault that stops the desk, we open it for up to 1 hour and write down why. We can only read the records, never change them. Every look is recorded, and the owner can see each one in the admin app.

  • The demo form on gyms.mettleup.fit asks for your mobile number. You may also give your gym's name, city, size and the software you use now. We use it only to call you back about MettleUp.
  • A friend's invite page sends your details only to that friend's gym (section 4B).
  • To stop spam, our forms keep a coded form of your internet address.

7. Delete your data

  1. From this phone: Settings → Your data → Erase or start over → "Erase all data". This removes everything the app holds on this phone, including progress photos.
  2. Your saved copy and MettleUp account: Settings → Your data → Backup and restore → "Delete my account". Both are deleted from our server at once; backup copies expire within 7 days. No app any more? Follow the steps at mettleup.fit/delete-account, or email us from your Google email address. If your Google account is also used for another MettleUp sign-in, we delete your saved workouts but keep that other sign-in working.
  3. What your gym has: leave the gym (Settings → Your gym → "Leave gym"). Your leaderboard numbers, trainer summary, machine use, shared workouts and questions are then deleted. Your membership, payments and visits stay with your gym, which may have to keep payment records by law. Ask your gym to erase the rest.

9. Under 18

10. How we protect it

Data travels encrypted. Each gym can reach only its own records. Only people the gym's owner adds can sign in to its admin app. MettleUp support can look only in a recorded window (section 4E). We keep backup copies so data is not lost.

If your data leaks, we tell you without delay, in the app or by message. We tell you what happened, what it means for you and what you can do. We report it to the Data Protection Board as the law requires. If it involves a gym's records, we also tell that gym within 24 hours.

Health data

Your body weight and measurements are health data. The GDPR protects them with extra rules, as "special category" data.

We keep them on our server only if you sign in with Google to keep a copy. Before you sign in, the app tells you that your body weight is saved. Signing in is your own clear choice, and it is how you give your explicit consent.

To take back your consent, delete those entries in the app, or delete your account (section 7). Entries you delete in the app are deleted on our server too.

Your progress photos never leave your phone, so we never get them. We never get your sleep, steps or heart rate either. The app only shows them on your phone or watch.

Data sent outside the EU and UK

Our main database is in Mumbai, India. Some companies in section 5 work in the USA and other countries. These countries may not protect data in the same way as EU and UK law.

So each company that handles your data for us does it under its data protection terms. These include the European Commission's "standard contractual clauses": contract terms the Commission approved to protect data sent abroad. For UK data, the UK's addendum to those clauses is used where the company's terms include it. Ask us if you want details.

  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct it or delete it.
  • You can ask for your data in a file you can take to another service. "Download my data" in the app makes that file for what is on your phone. For the data on our server, ask us.

Complaints: you can complain to the data protection authority of the EU or EEA country where you live or work. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). You can also write to us first, and we will try to fix it.

3. क्या आपके फोन पर ही रहता है

आप ऐप में जो कुछ भी लिखते हैं, वह आपके फोन पर ही रहता है, जब तक यह सूचना कुछ और न कहे। यानी आपके वर्कआउट, रूटीन, नोट्स, शरीर का वज़न, नाप और प्रोफ़ाइल। आपकी प्रोग्रेस फोटो कभी आपके फोन से बाहर नहीं जातीं।

"Download my data" (Settings → Your data → Backup and restore) आपके डेटा की एक फ़ाइल बनाता है। यह फ़ाइल तब तक आपके फोन पर रहती है, जब तक आप ख़ुद न चुनें कि उसे कहाँ रखना है। "Ask an AI about your workouts" (Settings → Your data) सिर्फ़ आपके फोन का शेयर मेन्यू खोलता है। ऐप आप ख़ुद चुनते हैं, और MettleUp ख़ुद कुछ नहीं भेजता।

C. Health Connect और आपकी घड़ी

Settings → Phone and watch → "Health apps" में आप चुनते हैं कि MettleUp क्या कर सकता है। Health Connect हर बार पहले आपसे इजाज़त माँगता है:

  • वर्कआउट सेव करना: हर पूरा हुआ वर्कआउट (एक्सरसाइज़, उसके शुरू और ख़त्म होने का समय, और एक्टिव कैलोरी) Health Connect में जाता है। फिर Google Health, Samsung Health और दूसरे ऐप उसे दिखा सकते हैं।
  • शरीर का वज़न: स्मार्ट वज़न मशीन या किसी दूसरे ऐप से आए वज़न आपके शरीर के वज़न वाले लॉग में जुड़ जाते हैं। उस लॉग के बाकी हिस्से की तरह, अगर आपने साइन इन किया है तो ये आपकी MettleUp कॉपी में होते हैं।
  • कदम (steps): आज के कदम उसी पेज पर दिखते हैं और रखे नहीं जाते।
  • "Sleep and workouts": ऐप आपके फोन पर पिछली रात की नींद (वह कब शुरू हुई, कब ख़त्म हुई, और नींद के अलग-अलग हिस्से) पढ़ता है। हल्के वर्कआउट के बाद, अगर आप 6 घंटे से कम सोए हों, तो ऐप आपको यह बताता है। नींद का डेटा न रखा जाता है, न उसका बैकअप होता है, न वह कहीं भेजा जाता है।

Health Connect का डेटा कभी आपके जिम, विज्ञापन देने वालों या किसी AI के पास नहीं जाता। MettleUp, Health Connect से मिली जानकारी का इस्तेमाल Health Connect Permissions Policy के हिसाब से करता है, जिसमें Limited Use की शर्तें भी शामिल हैं। बंद करने के लिए उसी पेज पर "Stop and remove MettleUp's access" दबाएँ, या Health Connect में इसे बदलें।

आपकी घड़ी: फोन और घड़ी के ऐप आपके अपने डिवाइसों पर Google Play services के ज़रिए आपका वर्कआउट सीधे एक-दूसरे को भेजते हैं। वर्कआउट के दौरान घड़ी अपने सेंसर से आपकी धड़कन (heart rate) दिखाती है। आपकी धड़कन न सेव होती है, न कहीं भेजी जाती है। फोन घड़ी वाले ऐप का वर्ज़न पढ़ता है, ताकि अपडेट तैयार होने पर आपको बता सके।

  • वह इंटरनेट पता जिससे कोई जिम बनाया गया, गलत इस्तेमाल रोकने के लिए।
  • MettleUp को भुगतान: प्लान, रकम, तारीख़, और आपका भेजा UPI या बैंक रेफ़रेंस नंबर।

  • पुरानी मेंबर-सूची इम्पोर्ट करना: कौन-सा कॉलम क्या है, यह समझने के लिए Google Gemini (या Gemini न चले तो Anthropic का Claude) कॉलम के नाम, 5 नमूना पंक्तियाँ और सरल गिनतियाँ देखता है। भेजने से पहले नाम, पते, मोबाइल नंबर, ईमेल और ID नंबर छिपा दिए जाते हैं। प्लान के नाम, रकम और तारीख़ें नहीं छिपाई जातीं। AI मेंबरों की कोई जानकारी वापस नहीं भेजता।
  • MettleUp सपोर्ट किसी जिम के रिकॉर्ड सिर्फ़ थोड़े समय के लिए मिली पहुँच में देखता है। आमतौर पर जिम का मालिक इसे 1 घंटे के लिए खोलता है। किसी इमरजेंसी में, जैसे कोई खराबी जो डेस्क का काम रोक दे, हम इसे ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा 1 घंटे के लिए खोलते हैं और कारण लिख लेते हैं। हम रिकॉर्ड सिर्फ़ पढ़ सकते हैं, कभी बदल नहीं सकते। हर बार देखना दर्ज होता है, और मालिक एडमिन ऐप में हर एक को देख सकता है।

  2. आपकी सेव की गई कॉपी और MettleUp खाता: Settings → Your data → Backup and restore → "Delete my account"। दोनों हमारे सर्वर से तुरंत डिलीट हो जाते हैं; बैकअप कॉपी 7 दिन के अंदर ख़त्म हो जाती हैं। ऐप अब फोन में नहीं है? mettleup.fit/delete-account पर दिए गए कदम पूरे करें, या अपने Google ईमेल पते से हमें ईमेल करें। अगर आपका Google खाता MettleUp में किसी दूसरे साइन इन के लिए भी इस्तेमाल होता है, तो हम आपके सेव किए वर्कआउट डिलीट कर देते हैं, पर वह दूसरा साइन इन चालू रखते हैं।
  3. आपके जिम के पास जो है: जिम छोड़ दें (Settings → Your gym → "Leave gym")। तब आपके लीडरबोर्ड के नंबर, ट्रेनर के लिए सार, मशीनों का इस्तेमाल, शेयर किए गए वर्कआउट और सवाल डिलीट हो जाते हैं। आपकी मेंबरशिप, भुगतान और विज़िट आपके जिम के पास रहते हैं, और जिम को कानून के हिसाब से भुगतान के रिकॉर्ड रखने पड़ सकते हैं। बाकी मिटवाने के लिए अपने जिम से कहें।

8. आपकी पसंद और अधिकार

आप कोई भी शेयरिंग कभी भी बंद कर सकते हैं, उतनी ही आसानी से जितनी आसानी से आपने उसे चालू किया था (स्विच सेक्शन 4B की टेबल में हैं)। बंद करने से नई शेयरिंग रुक जाती है। जहाँ सेक्शन 6 ऐसा कहता है, वहाँ जो पहले शेयर हो चुका है, वह भी तुरंत मिटा दिया जाता है। उसके बाद कुछ फ़ीचर काम करना बंद कर देते हैं।

आप हमसे यह भी कह सकते हैं कि हम:

  • हमारे पास आपके बारे में जो डेटा है, उसका सारांश दें, और बताएँ कि हमने उसे किसके साथ शेयर किया;
  • उसे सही या पूरा करें;
  • कानून जहाँ इजाज़त दे, वहाँ उसे मिटा दें;
  • अगर आपकी मृत्यु हो जाए या आप ख़ुद कुछ न कर सकें, तो आपके चुने हुए किसी व्यक्ति को आपकी जगह ये अधिकार इस्तेमाल करने दें।

contact@mettleup.fit पर लिखें। हमें वह Google ईमेल या मोबाइल नंबर बताएँ जिससे आपका डेटा जुड़ा है, ताकि हम उसे ढूँढ सकें। हम 30 दिन के अंदर जवाब देते हैं। अपने जिम के रिकॉर्ड के लिए आप सीधे अपने जिम से भी कह सकते हैं। अगर हम आपकी शिकायत हल न करें, तो आप भारत के डेटा संरक्षण बोर्ड (Data Protection Board of India) से शिकायत कर सकते हैं।

9. 18 साल से कम

MettleUp सिर्फ़ 18 साल या उससे ज़्यादा उम्र के लोगों के लिए है। अगर हमें पता चले कि हमारे पास 18 साल से कम उम्र के किसी व्यक्ति का डेटा है, तो हम उसे मिटा देते हैं।

10. हम इसे कैसे सुरक्षित रखते हैं

डेटा एन्क्रिप्ट होकर (encrypted) भेजा जाता है। हर जिम सिर्फ़ अपने रिकॉर्ड तक पहुँच सकता है। किसी जिम के एडमिन ऐप में सिर्फ़ वही लोग साइन इन कर सकते हैं, जिन्हें जिम का मालिक जोड़ता है। MettleUp सपोर्ट सिर्फ़ थोड़े समय के लिए मिली पहुँच में देख सकता है, और यह दर्ज होता है (सेक्शन 4E)। हम बैकअप कॉपी रखते हैं, ताकि डेटा खो न जाए।

अगर आपका डेटा लीक हो जाए, तो हम बिना देर किए आपको ऐप में या संदेश से बताते हैं। हम बताते हैं कि क्या हुआ, आपके लिए इसका क्या मतलब है, और आप क्या कर सकते हैं। कानून के अनुसार हम इसकी रिपोर्ट डेटा संरक्षण बोर्ड को करते हैं। अगर इसमें किसी जिम के रिकॉर्ड शामिल हों, तो हम उस जिम को भी 24 घंटे के अंदर बताते हैं।
