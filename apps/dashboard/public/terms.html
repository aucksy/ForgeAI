MettleUp terms of use

These terms are between you and MettleUp, run by Aakash Pahuja, a sole proprietor in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India ("we"). Part A is for everyone who uses the MettleUp app. Part B is for gyms and their staff who use the admin app. Our privacy notice explains what happens to your data.

A. For members (the MettleUp app)

1. Agreeing to these terms

By using the app, you agree to these terms. When you join a gym in the app, you also tick a box to say so. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. You must be 18 or older

The app is only for people aged 18 or over.

3. What MettleUp is

MettleUp is a free workout log for your phone and watch. If your gym uses MettleUp, the app also links you to your gym: your plan, check-in, shop, messages and your trainer's workouts. We keep improving the app. Features may change, and sometimes something may not work.

4. Your health

MettleUp is not a doctor or a medical service. Suggested weights, plans and tips are general guidance, not medical advice. Exercise carries a risk of injury. Train within your limits, stop if you feel pain, and ask a doctor before you start if you have a health condition. Heart rate, calories, sleep and step numbers come from your devices and may not be exact.

5. Your account and your data

  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you do not sign in, your workouts are only on your phone, so keep your phone backed up.
  • Your workouts are yours. You can download them (Settings → Your data → Backup and restore → "Download my data") or delete them at any time.
  • Keep your phone and your Google account safe. Do not use another person's account or gym code.

6. Your gym

  • Your membership, prices, payments and refunds are between you and your gym. The app shows what your gym records.
  • Shop orders are with your gym. The gym sets prices and stock.
  • If you pay your gym online in the app, the payment goes to your gym through its payment company. Their terms also apply. We do not take or hold your money.
  • Some features exist only if your gym switches them on.

7. What you share with others

Some things you write reach other people: questions to a trainer, messages to your gym, and, if you share workouts with gym mates, your bio and those workouts. You still own them. You let us store them and show them to the people you chose. Do not post anything abusive, false or unlawful. Do not post another person's private details. Only give a friend's details in "Bring a friend" if they agreed. We may remove anything that breaks these rules.

8. Fair use

Do not try to see other people's data, break or overload the app, copy it, or get around its limits. Do not pretend to be someone else.

9. Other services

Google sign-in, Health Connect, Google Play, WhatsApp and payment companies have their own terms. Those terms apply when you use them through MettleUp.

10. Ending

You can stop using the app, leave your gym or delete your account at any time. We may stop your access if you misuse the app or break these terms.

11. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep MettleUp running and your data safe, but we cannot promise it will never fail. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for indirect losses. If you did not sign in and you lose your phone, we cannot bring your workouts back. Nothing in these terms takes away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 or any other law that cannot be set aside.

12. Changes to these terms

Before a change takes effect, we tell you in the app. If you keep using the app after that, the new terms apply. If you do not agree, stop using it and you can delete your account.

13. Law and complaints

Indian law applies, and the courts in Jaipur decide disputes. You can still go to a consumer commission where you live. For a complaint, write to our Grievance Officer, Aakash Pahuja, at contact@mettleup.fit. We answer within 30 days.

If one part of these terms is not valid, the rest still applies.

B. For gyms (the admin app)

This part is a business agreement between MettleUp and the gym. The gym's owner accepts it by ticking "I agree" in the admin app, for the whole gym and all its branches. Our data agreement is part of it.

1. The person who agrees

The person who ticks "I agree" must be the gym's owner, or someone allowed to agree for the gym.

2. Free trial

Every new gym gets 3 months free. You do not need to pay or give card details. We may give a longer trial. When the trial ends, we ask you to pick a plan and pay. Section 5 says what happens if you do not.

3. Plans and payment

  • Plans, prices and what each plan includes are on gyms.mettleup.fit.
  • You pay from the admin app: Settings → "Pay MettleUp", by UPI or bank transfer. Then you send us the payment reference. Your plan starts or extends when we confirm the payment.

4. Refunds

Once a paid plan is on, payments are not refunded. This is true for monthly and yearly payments, and for the one-time payment of a founding gym. If you paid twice by mistake, or paid more than the price, we refund the extra amount to the same account. You can stop at any time, with no fee. A plan then runs until the end of the period you paid for, and does not renew.

5. If you do not pay

This covers a trial that ended without a plan, and a late payment. We tell you in the admin app first. After that, we may switch off the paid features step by step. We never delete your records because a payment is late, and you can always see and export them.

6. Your members' data

  • Your gym's records about members are your gym's. Your gym is in charge of them, and MettleUp only stores them for you. (Under India's data law, your gym is the "Data Fiduciary" and MettleUp is your "Data Processor".)
  • The data agreement lists what each of us must do, including telling your members (it has a short notice you can copy).

7. Your staff

  • Only add people who need access, and remove them when they leave. Removing a person signs them out on every device at once.
  • Each person uses only their own sign-in and never shares a PIN or code.
  • Staff use members' details only for the gym's work, during and after their job.
  • Enter payments as they happen. Never change a receipt to hide money. Only the owner can cancel a receipt, with the true reason. Every receipt shows who took the money.
  • Tell us at once if a phone or computer with the admin app is lost, or if data looks wrong or exposed.

8. Online payments from your members

If you connect your own payment company account (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU or PhonePe), your members pay you through that account. Your agreement with that company applies. We only ask it to make payment links and to tell us when they are paid. We keep your connection keys locked and never show them again.

9. Getting your data out, and ending

  • The owner can download all of the gym's data at any time, from Settings in the admin app.
  • You can stop at any time. You can also ask us to delete your gym. We delete its records within 30 days of your request (section 9 of the data agreement).
  • We may end this agreement with 30 days' notice, or at once for serious misuse. You can download your data during the notice.

10. Ready-made plans

MettleUp gives you ready-made diet and workout plans to start from. They are general examples for healthy adults. They are not medical or diet advice for any one person. You decide which plans to send, and you can change, delete or add plans at any time. When your gym sends a plan to a member, your gym is responsible for checking it suits that member: their health, injuries, allergies and food choices. Send members to a doctor or a qualified dietitian when needed. As far as the law allows, MettleUp is not responsible for any injury, illness or loss from a plan your gym sends.

11. The service

We work to keep the admin app running and to fix faults quickly, but we cannot promise it will never stop. The desk keeps working without internet on a device that is already signed in. If something breaks, note it on paper, carry on, and tell us.

12. Responsibility

As far as the law allows, MettleUp's total responsibility to your gym for any claim is limited to what your gym paid MettleUp in the 12 months before the claim. We are not responsible for lost profits or indirect losses. This does not limit what the law does not allow us to limit.

13. Changes

Before a new version applies, we tell the owner in the admin app and ask them to accept it again. The date at the top shows the latest version.

14. Law

Indian law applies, and the courts in Jaipur decide disputes. Contact: Aakash Pahuja, contact@mettleup.fit.
