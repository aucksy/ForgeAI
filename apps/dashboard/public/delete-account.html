Delete your MettleUp account

This page helps you delete your account in the MettleUp workout app (by Aakash Pahuja, on Google Play as "MettleUp: Gym Workout Tracker") and the workouts saved with it.

You only have an account if you tapped Continue with Google in the app. If you never did, nothing is saved on our server: your workouts are only on your phone, and there is nothing for us to delete.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the MettleUp app and go to Settings, then Your data, then Backup and restore.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with Delete account.

We delete your account and everything saved on our server at once. The workouts on your phone stay. To remove them too, uninstall the app.

No app any more? Email us

Email contact@mettleup.fit from the Google email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your workouts, with every exercise and set.
  • Your routines and routine folders, and the exercises you made yourself.
  • Your rest-timer lengths and where your personal records start.
  • Your body weight and body measurements.
  • The dates of your progress photos. The photos themselves stay on your phone, because we never save them on our server.
  • Your profile, and the email, name and picture link we got from Google.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backup copies: we remove every backup copy of your data within 7 days of deletion.
  • If you also run or work at a gym with the same Google account (as an owner or trainer): we delete your saved workouts but keep that sign-in, so the gym's app keeps working.
  • If you joined a gym in the app: your name, mobile number, check-ins, shop orders and messages are part of the gym's own records, and the gym keeps them. Ask your gym if you want them removed. When you leave the gym in the app, we erase your mobile number from them.
  • Your trainers' weekly summary of your workouts is deleted when you turn off Share my workouts with my trainers or leave the gym.
