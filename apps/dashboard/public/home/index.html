Directions for use

Every visit, a member sees last time's numbers, beats them, and comes back to do it again. That loop is what keeps them at your gym.

  1. They join with your QR poster

    One scan at the desk and the app wears your gym's name and colours.

  2. They train with your plan

    Your trainer's plan arrives by itself. Beside every set: last time's numbers and the kg to beat.

  3. Records fall, they come back

    A new record shows the moment it happens. The weekly goal, milestones and a monthly report give them a reason for the next visit.
