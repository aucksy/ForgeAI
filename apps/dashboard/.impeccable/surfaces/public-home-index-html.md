Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of independent Indian gyms (Jaipur first) on an Android phone,
evening, between rushes or after closing; WhatsApp-first; English or Hindi. Job: believe that the
member app keeps members coming (they see their own progress), that the shop earns money and messages
reach every member; then start the free 3-month trial (/start), or leave a WhatsApp number for a demo

## Direction contract
THESIS: ForgeAI is sold the way the owner already sells his best product: as a tub on his own shelf.
The page is a supplement label for "member retention", with a facts panel, a flavour band and
directions for use. It refuses the dark SaaS split hero with a phone mock and a card grid.
OWN-WORLD: matte black tub ground; a saturated flavour band that is the gym's own colour (default
ForgeAI orange); heavy condensed caps for the brand line and claims; a black-on-white facts panel with
thick and hairline rules in a tight grotesk; seal and burst stickers; price-tag call-outs on leader
lines for the shop. Raises: per-character number roll for every figure (from the split-flap board);
hand call-outs on leader lines naming parts of the app (from the magazine spread); one percussive
"NEW RECORD" slam scaled by loudness (from the futurist book); hierarchy by colour field, never by
extra weights (from the glazier wall).
STORY (R-97, 24 Oct 2026): see the offer in one line and the free-trial door, play with the label (type
your gym's name, pick its colour, watch the member app wear it), see a member's workout make progress,
the shop, the notices and the lock screen, the facts, the desk work, moving in privately (AI import),
the pack sizes and the founding pack, then the back: start the trial, or a demo call.

with its no-card line, visible without scrolling on a phone and a 1366x768 laptop; the flavour band and
