# Surface brief: a gym's own free web page (join.mettleup.fit/<gym>)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a person in an Indian city (Jaipur first), 16-40, on an Android phone in daylight or
on the sofa at night, who tapped a link the gym shared on WhatsApp or Instagram, or found it on Google. Job:
decide in one screen that this gym is near, open at their hours and affordable, then tap "Become a member"
(the existing join-online flow, pay at the desk) or send a question that lands in the owner's Enquiries.
Every fact comes from the gym's own records (name, logo, colour, plans and prices, timings, address, photos,
a short about); the template is the same for every gym and only the gym's colour, logo and photos change it.
No AI helper on the page (on hold, R-519). Unattended run: interview and decision page skipped under the
owner's standing rule; assigned direction (seed d49bc384, candidate 5) built as dealt. Code-led (no image
generation used).

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page is the gym's own shopfront, the way an Indian gym actually announces itself: a hand-painted
signboard over a rolling shutter, then the painted rate board and timings board by the door. It refuses the
category template (a dark hero photo, a headline, three feature cards, a pricing grid).
OWN-WORLD: the signboard is a flat field of the gym's own colour, lettered in a condensed Indian signboard face
(Teko, Devanagari-ready) with the sign-painter's flat block shadow; bilingual board headers (FEES · फीस); a
steel rolling shutter of horizontal ridges; white enamel tin boards with a colour header strip and painted
rupee numerals; a lime-washed wall ground; no gradients except the shutter's metal ridges.
Raises: one horizon rhythm (the shutter ridges pace every section, from the city-pop sleeve); open / closing
soon / closed painted onto the board itself, never as chrome (from the terminal); depth only by overlapping
flat layers (from the papercut).
STORY: the visitor sees the gym's name and whether it is open now, the shutter rolls up on the floor inside,
they read the fees and the timings like the boards at the door, find it on the map, and join or ask.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the signboard owns the top: tin logo badge, the gym name in huge painted letters, area line,
the open-now plate, and two actions on the board (Become a member, Ask a question). The shutter's lower edge
and the first photo show under it, so the page visibly continues.
FORM: hand-painted shop signboard and rolling shutter; position 5 on the grounded list (1 gym flex hoarding,
2 Instagram profile with highlights, 3 laminated fee chart, 4 bumper plates, 5 painted shutter signboard,
6 membership ID card, 7 newspaper insert); seed key d49bc384.
