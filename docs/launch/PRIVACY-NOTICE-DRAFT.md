## ForgeAI privacy notice

**Who we are.** ForgeAI is a workout app for members and a desk app for gyms, run by [Aakash
Pahuja / company name], [address]. Questions or complaints about your data: **[name], [email]**.
We answer within 90 days, and usually within a week.

**Two kinds of data, two different owners.**
1. **Your gym's records about you** (name, mobile, date of birth if given, plan, payments,
   receipts, visits, shop orders, messages with the desk). Your **gym** decides what it keeps and
   why (to run your membership). We store and process it **for the gym**, under a written
   agreement. For these records, ask your gym first; you can also write to us.
2. **Your own workouts** (exercises, sets, weights, body weight, progress photos). These stay
   **on your phone**. We do not receive them, and your gym does not see them, unless you choose a
   feature that shares something (each such feature asks you first).

**What we keep on our server, and why**

| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Gym records listed above | So the gym can run memberships, receipts and check-ins | While the gym uses ForgeAI; deleted within 30 days after it leaves |
| Your name and mobile when you join a gym in the app | So the gym knows who checked in or ordered | Until you leave the gym in the app, or ask us |
| Gym staff names and mobiles | Staff sign-in | While they work there |
| Technical logs (errors, sign-ins) | To keep the service working and safe | Up to 1 year |

We do **not** sell data, show ads, or use your data to profile you.

**Where it is kept.** On servers in **Mumbai, India** (Supabase). The desk app is delivered
through Cloudflare. App downloads come from Google Play or GitHub. [Crash reports: Sentry, once
added.] These companies process data only to provide their service to us.

**Your choices and rights.** You can: see what the gym holds about you (ask the desk or us);
correct it; withdraw consent for anything optional (it stops from then on); leave the gym in the
app (your link to the gym is removed); ask us to delete the server data about you (write to the
contact above; we reply within 7 days) [an in-app "Delete my data" button is planned]; complain to
us, and then to India's Data Protection Board.

**Under 18.** [Per inbox #29: the member app is for people aged 18 and over during the trial.]

**If something goes wrong.** If your data is exposed, we tell your gym within 24 hours, and you
are told directly (in the app, WhatsApp or SMS) what happened and what to do.

**Changes.** We tell you in the app before any change that affects you.
