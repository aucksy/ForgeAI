# MettleUp website words, new version (24 Oct 2026, R-112)

For https://mettleup.fit (the home page for gym owners). Written with the member-words rules, then checked line by
line by Google Gemini (gemini-3-flash-preview, free key, public text only). Each line is the better of the two, or a
merge; "(G)" marks a line taken from Gemini, "(G+)" a Gemini line with a fact put back. Hindi follows in the build.

**What changed from the old page:** the lock-screen rest-timer section is gone (owner: not worth pushing). New sections
for the things that are really ours: the branded member app, trainer plans, who is slipping away, the desk that works
offline, several branches. The price table now leads with the exclusive items, each marked.

**Gemini lines NOT used (facts wrong or promises we can't keep):** "train every day" (not what the app asks);
"the AI only reads column names" (it also sees 5 sample rows); "the only software that uses AI to import" (we checked
12, so we say 12); "AI identifies the names and fees automatically" (drops the check before saving); "no hidden fees"
(empty words); "pay once and never pay again" (drops "one branch" and the AI pack being extra).

## 1. Hero
- **Headline:** Members stay longer when they see progress. (G)
- **Sub:** MettleUp gives you the desk software to run your gym, and gives every member a workout app in your gym's name. (G+)
- **Button:** Start free for 3 months
- **Under the button:** No card, no setup fee. Your gym is ready in 2 minutes.

## 2. Your gym's own app
- **Headline:** An app with your gym's name and logo. (G)
- **Sub:** Members scan your QR poster once. From then on, the app opens in your gym's name, logo and colours.
- **Point:** Your members never pay to use the app. (G)
- **Point (exclusive tag "Usually extra"):** Most gym software charges more for a branded member app. Here it is in every pack.

## 3. Progress that brings them back
- **Headline:** A reason to come back, every set.
- **Sub:** The app shows members what they lifted last time, so they try to beat it today. (G)
- **Point:** A "New record" message the moment a member lifts more than ever before. (G+)
- **Point:** Members see their body change in photos and charts, week by week. (G)
- **Point:** A weekly goal and a monthly report help members build a gym habit. (G)

## 4. Trainer plans
- **Headline:** Your trainer's plan, in every member's phone.
- **Sub:** Your trainer makes a plan once, with starting weights, and sends it to one member or a whole membership plan. It appears in their app by itself.
- **Point:** After they check in, members start today's workout with one tap. (G)

## 5. Who is slipping away
- **Headline:** See who stopped coming, before they quit. (G)
- **Sub:** Every morning, a list shows who is missing their usual visits. Each member is compared with their own routine. (G+)
- **Point:** Renewals: who ends today, and in 1-3, 4-7 and 8-15 days.
- **Point:** A WhatsApp message is already typed for each one. Tap, check, send.
- **Point:** The app can also remind members by itself. You switch each reminder on or off.

## 6. Works without internet
- **Headline:** Run your desk even when the internet is down. (G)
- **Sub:** Check members in and take payments with no internet. Everything is sent when the internet is back.
- **Point:** Use it on the desk phone or the computer. (G)

## 7. AI import (exclusive tag "Only here")
- **Headline:** Move your member list from Excel, with AI. (G)
- **Sub:** Any sheet, any column names, even a GGMS export. AI works out which column is which. You check every member and rupee before anything is saved.
- **Point:** Private: the AI sees only the column names and 5 sample rows, with mobile numbers hidden.
- **Point:** Only here: none of the 12 gym software products we checked in India and abroad (Oct 2026) reads any Excel layout with AI.

## 8. Shop
- **Headline:** Sell supplements through your own app. (G)
- **Sub:** Members order in the app and pay at your desk, in cash or UPI. Stock goes down by itself. (G+)

## 9. Notices
- **Headline:** Send free notices to every member. (G)
- **Sub:** Write it once. It reaches every member's app inbox in seconds, and you see how many read it. No SMS or WhatsApp cost.
