# Gym visit — question sheet (about 45 minutes)

For the launch plan (`docs/LAUNCH-PLAN.md`, F1-F2). Take this on your phone. Write the answers
in the chat or a photo of your notes; Claude turns them into the plan.

## A. The desk (10 min) — look, then ask
1. What does the desk use today: a computer, a laptop, a phone, a tablet? Which browser?
2. Is the internet at the desk reliable? Does it drop at peak hours? Is there a backup (phone hotspot)?
3. Is there a receipt printer? Which kind (A4, thermal 58/80 mm)? Do members want a printed receipt?
4. Is there a fingerprint or face machine at the door? Which brand? Do they want to keep it?

## B. The people (10 min)
5. Owner's name, and who decides on the switch. Who will be our **champion** at the desk?
6. Staff: names, roles (desk, trainer, manager), shifts and hours. Who handles money?
7. Gym opening hours and the rush hours.

## C. Members and money (10 min)
8. How many active members? How many joined or renewed last month?
9. Plans and prices (monthly, 3, 6, 12 months, personal training, couple or family plans, offers).
10. How do members pay: cash / UPI / card, roughly what share each?
11. Is the gym **GST-registered**? (If yes: the GSTIN, for receipts at 5%.)
12. How many come through **FitPass / cultpass**, roughly per week?
13. Any group classes with fixed times?
14. Any members under 18? Roughly how many?

## D. GGMS, the old software (10 min)
15. What can GGMS export? Ask them to try: members list, plans, dues, payment history, attendance.
    Excel is best; a PDF or a backup file also helps. **Bring a copy back** (or have it sent to you).
16. Bring 2 sample receipts (a photo is fine) and 1 sample member card if they have one.
17. What does GGMS cost them, and when is it due for renewal? (They keep it until week 8.)

## E. What they want (5 min)
18. The #1 daily headache at the desk today?
19. Would members install a gym app? What would make them?
20. What would make them say "this is better than GGMS" after 2 months?

## Agree before leaving
- The pilot runs 8 weeks; GGMS stays on until then. The price after the pilot: only what you
  decided in the owner inbox (#30); do not promise anything else.
- A side-by-side run of about 2 weeks, where the desk enters everything twice.
- A date for the training session(s), one per shift.
- A WhatsApp group: gym owner, champion, you.
