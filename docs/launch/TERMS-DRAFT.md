## A. For members (ForgeAI app)

1. **What it is.** A workout log and coach-free training app, plus links to your gym (plan, check-in,
   shop, trainer's workouts) when you join one. It is a **beta**: things may change or break.
2. **Not medical advice.** Suggested weights and plans are general guidance. Train within your
   limits; ask a doctor or trainer if unsure. You use the app at your own risk.
3. **Your account.** Keep your phone and gym code safe. Do not use someone else's gym code or
   account.
4. **Your gym.** Your membership, prices, payments and refunds are between you and your gym. The
   app shows what the gym records; questions about them go to the gym.
5. **Shop orders.** Orders are placed with your gym and paid at the desk. The gym sets prices and
   stock.
6. **Fair use.** No misuse: no attempts to see other people's data, break the app, or send abusive
   messages to the gym.
7. **Your data.** See the privacy notice. Workouts stay on your phone; keep your own backup.
8. **Ending.** You can stop using the app or leave your gym at any time. We may end access for
   misuse.
9. **Liability.** The app is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for
   indirect losses, or for lost workout data kept only on your phone.
10. **Changes and contact.** We tell you in the app before changes that affect you. Contact:
    [name], [email]. Indian law applies; courts in [Jaipur].

## B. For gym staff (ForgeAI admin app)

1. **Who may use it.** Only people the gym owner adds. Use only your own sign-in; never share a
   sign-in code.
2. **Members' data is confidential.** Use it only for the gym's work (memberships, payments,
   check-ins, the shop). Do not copy, export or share it for anything else, or after you leave.
3. **Money records.** Enter payments as they happen. Never delete or alter a receipt to hide money;
   use "Cancel receipt" with the true reason (owner only). Every receipt shows who took it.
4. **Report problems.** Tell the owner at once if a phone or computer with the app is lost, or if
   you see data that looks wrong or exposed.
5. **Beta.** If something breaks, write it on paper and carry on; report it in the support group.
6. **Ending.** The owner can remove your access at any time; it stops immediately on all devices.
