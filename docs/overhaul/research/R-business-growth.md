# R — Business growth view ("Your growth" page)

Short web research, 29 Oct 2026. Purpose: inform the "Your growth" page (12 months of money,
members, renewals, losses, visits, plus Before/Since MettleUp). Vendor pages are marketing; treat
their claims as claims. Every number below has a source link; where no credible number exists,
this note says so.

## 1. How other gym software shows growth

- **ABC Glofox** — reports grouped into sales, payments, members, staff, classes. A "member
  movement" view shows new sign-ups, net movement and members lost together; revenue reports cover
  sales, failed payments, money owed and scheduled revenue. [S1]
  Its owner blog defines monthly retention as (members at month end − new that month) ÷ members at
  start × 100. [S2]
- **Mindbody** — Insights > Reports > Clients > Visits & Retention; "new client growth" and
  "client retention" under client volume; a "No Return" report; AI "Clients at Risk" on all plans;
  "Comparative Analytics" (benchmarks against similar businesses) only on higher plans. CSV/PDF
  export. [S3][S4]
- **PushPress** — markets churn reduction: at-risk member list, a churn report (2022), a free
  retention calculator. Publishes monthly churn tiers (see section 4). [S5][S6]
- **Wodify** — "Insights" dashboard: 90-day client retention compared with an industry standard
  and how it changed over time; ~28 report tables; custom dashboard. [S7]
- **GymMaster** — KPI report for any period, compare periods (e.g. this January vs last January);
  dashboard has a "membership movement" graph; its retention rate has been defined as members who
  re-signed within 28 days of expiry. [S8][S9]
- **ABC Evo / Ignite** — "Evo Insights" business-intelligence add-on; no public KPI list found. [S10]
- **Zen Planner** — no public description of its growth reports found in this pass.
- **India: FitnessForce** — "50+ reports" on sales, marketing, inventory; India/GCC focus. [S11]
  **Wellyx** — tracks memberships, payments, cancellations, renewal reminders, "performance
  reports"; no detail public. [S12] **Gymshim** — public listing describes member workout
  reviews, not business growth reports. [S13]
- **Pattern:** everyone has *many reports* and a *dashboard of today's numbers*. Only Wodify
  (retention over time vs a benchmark) and GymMaster (period vs period) show a trend story. None
  found offers a "before you used us vs since" comparison or a one-sentence plain-words verdict.

## 2. Numbers owners use to judge growth

- **Active member count trend** and **new vs lost members** (net movement) — the core view in
  Glofox and GymMaster. [S1][S2][S9]
- **Revenue / recurring revenue** and money owed / failed payments. [S1]
- **Retention / churn rate** — the industry's headline number (HFA benchmarks it every year). [S14]
- **Renewal rate** — GymMaster's re-sign-within-28-days definition is the closest match to ours
  (renewed within 15 days). [S8]
- **Visits per member** — the leading signal: PushPress says members with 12+ check-ins a month
  have ~2% chance of quitting next month vs ~20% with one check-in (their own data). [S5]
  Research by Hillsdon / The Retention People names visit frequency among the strongest predictors
  of retention, and a slow first visit (more than two weeks) as a warning. [S15]
- **Member lifetime value** — PushPress calculator projects it from churn. [S5] Useful for sales
  talk; not needed on the first version of our page.

## 3. What owners complain about

- **Numbers don't match between reports** — Mindbody reviewers say figures don't line up across
  reports and that they audit reports because they don't trust them. [S16][S17]
- **Fixed, "canned" reports**; several reports needed to answer one question. [S16][S17]
- **Can't even tell how many active members they have; poor Excel export** (iGo Figure review). [S18]
- **Reporting "clunky, incomplete, difficult to use"** (general buyer guide). [S19]
- **Owners aren't trained to read reports**; vendors themselves warn about vanity numbers. [S20]
- Not found: credible Reddit (r/gymowner) threads on this in a quick search — no claim made.

## 4. Benchmarks (only numbers with a source)

- **Annual retention ~66% (66.4%)** — HFA 2025 Fitness Industry Benchmarking Report, 175
  companies, 17,000+ facilities, 27 countries; each company uses its own formula. Quoted by
  PushPress and Uptivo; the full report is members-only. [S5][S21][S14]
- **Monthly churn tiers (PushPress, boutique gyms, no external source):** under 3% elite, 3–4%
  strong, under 5% good, 5–6%+ "fix the bucket". [S5]
- **US churn in 2025 at a "decade low"** — HFA says so but the public page gives no number. [S22]
- **Older "71.4% retention" (IHRSA)** — widely repeated, traces to mid-2010s data; do not use. [S21]
- **"~50% quit within 6 months", "30–40% churn a year"** — on vendor blogs with no primary study;
  do not show. [S23]
- **India: no credible published figure found.** Only an unsourced aggregator line and one vendor
  case study (a Bangalore chain renewal 45% → 68%). Neither is fit to show on screen. [S24]
- Our measures differ from all of these (we count renewals within 15 days; HFA lets each operator
  pick its formula), so any benchmark on screen needs a "measured differently" note.

## 5. Ideas to make our page clearer than theirs

1. **One plain sentence on top**, computed from the data, e.g. "Since you started with MettleUp
   (Mar 2026) you collect ₹X more a month on average and keep N more members." If data is thin,
   say that instead of a number.
2. **A "Started MettleUp" marker line on every chart**, with the before months shaded — the
   before/after story without reading a table. Nobody above does this.
3. **A fair-comparison note under Before vs Since**: how many months on each side, that "before"
   comes from imported records (may be incomplete), and that seasons differ (Jan rush). Each number
   one tap from its definition, and every total on the page adds up to the same figure as the CSV —
   the top complaint is numbers that don't match.
