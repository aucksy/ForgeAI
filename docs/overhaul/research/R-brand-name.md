- **Top pick: Repghar.** Runner-up: **Kilojeet.**
- All five names on the shortlist have **.com, .in and .app free**, at the normal price (no premium). GoDaddy India shows the standard price for each one.

## The shortlist (5)

**1. Repghar** ("rep" + "ghar", home): "the home of your reps"
- Free: repghar.com, repghar.in, repghar.app. Normal price (GoDaddy confirmed all three).
- Why it fits: it covers both halves of the pitch. Reps = progress. Ghar = the gym as a place you come back to (retention).
- Clash: none found. No app with this name on Google Play and nothing on the web. Many unrelated "Ghar" apps exist (it is a common word). Rough trademark pre-check: no exact match. Nearest marks: "Rephair", "Ramghar".
- Hindi: रेपघर. Natural, warm, no bad meaning. English speakers may say "rep-gar", which is fine.

**2. Kilojeet** ("kilo" + "jeet", win): "win the kilos"
- Free: kilojeet.com, kilojeet.in, kilojeet.app. Normal price (GoDaddy confirmed .com and .app).
- Why it fits: it ties straight to the "kg to beat" screen. It also sounds like a Punjabi first name (Kuljeet, Amarjeet), so it can become a mascot: "Kilojeet says: 2.5 kg to beat."
- Clash: none found on Play or the web. Nearest trademarks: "Kilobeaters", "Koldjeet". They are different words.
- Hindi: किलोजीत. Friendly and a little funny. It works for both goals: winning kilos on the bar, or beating kilos off the body.

**3. RepAhead**: "stay a rep ahead"
- Free: repahead.com, repahead.in, repahead.app. Normal price (GoDaddy confirmed .com).
- Why it fits: plain English, clearly about progress. It is the safest choice if you sell outside the Hindi belt later.
- Clash: no app with this name. "Reppa: Train Together" (a gym app on Play) is near but different. Similar trademarks already exist ("Stepahead", "Getahead", "Keepahead"). So this name is **weaker to protect** than a made-up word.
- Hindi: no Hindi meaning. Easy to say, no th or v/w trap.

**4. AglaSet** ("agla set" = "next set")
- Free: aglaset.com, aglaset.in, aglaset.app. Normal price (GoDaddy confirmed .com).
- Why it fits: it is what people actually say between sets, and it carries "come back for the next one".
- Clash: nothing in fitness. "Aglet" (an AR phone game) and a trademark "Maglaset" exist.
- Hindi: अगला सेट. Perfect in Hindi. English readers may stumble ("a-glass-et"), so always write it AglaSet, with a capital S.

**5. IronAdda** ("adda" = your regular hangout)
- Free: ironadda.com, ironadda.in, ironadda.app. Normal price (GoDaddy confirmed .com).
- Why it fits: the gym as the place you keep coming back to. Tough and very Indian.
- Clash: no app or company with this exact name. But **"Iron Addict" is a gym chain in Kolkata** (on FITPASS), and it sounds close. "Adda" is common in brands (Adda247).
- Hindi: आयरन अड्डा. Warm, but in news Hindi "adda" can also mean a den, as in a den of crooks. It also sounds like a gym's own name, which may confuse owners whose app shows *their* gym's name.

## Before you buy

- **Domains go fast.** Two names on this list were registered by someone in the last 4 months (repkar.com in July 2026, gymyaar.com in June 2026). Once you pick, buy the .com and the .in the same day. The .app is optional.
- **Trademark search: the official one was not done.** The IP India public search needs a captcha. I only did a rough pre-check on QuickCompany, an unofficial copy of the Indian register. It found no exact match for any of the five names. Before the logo, run the official IP India search, or ask a trademark lawyer (usually a few thousand rupees). Check classes 9 (app/software), 35 (business management), 41 (fitness and training) and 42 (online software). Then file in your own name.
- Before you pay, also check that the Instagram and YouTube handles are free.

## How it was checked

- Domains: each registry's own RDAP lookup. Verisign for .com, NIXI for .in, Google Registry for .app. "Not found" = free. For a taken .com, the nameservers show whether it is parked or listed for sale (Afternic, Sedo, Atom, Dan, HugeDomains/NameBright and so on).
- Normal vs premium price: GoDaddy India search results for the shortlisted names.
- Clashes: Google Play search (India store) and web search for each shortlisted name.

### How it was checked (23 Oct)

- Domains: each registry's own RDAP lookup, the same method as 22 Oct. Verisign for .com, NIXI for .in, Google Registry for .app. "Not found" = free. For a taken .com, the nameservers show whether it is parked or listed for sale (Afternic, Atom, NameBright/HugeDomains, BrandBucket, Sedo, Dan, ParkLogic, dns-parking and so on).
- Normal vs premium price: GoDaddy India's own search, read from inside a normal browser tab (GoDaddy blocks plain automated requests).
- Clashes: Google Play India search for each finalist (joined and split, e.g. "liftchase" and "lift chase"), web search, and QuickCompany for Indian trademarks.
