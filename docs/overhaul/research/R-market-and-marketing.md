# MettleUp: how big the money is, who we beat, and how to get the first 50 gyms (6 Nov 2026)

Research only. Nothing was changed, sent or posted. Prices come from R-pricing-jaipur.md and are not
reopened here. Every outside number has a link in Sources ([M1], [M2] ...). Anything marked
**(guess)** is mine. Review data on Indian gym software is thin: most products have under 20 public
reviews, so the "what owners say" parts are weaker than the price parts.

---

## 1. Summary (one page)

**The market is big enough. Jaipur alone is enough to break even.**
- India has about 46,500 gyms and fitness centres [M1]. 80% of them are "value" gyms, the small
  local kind we sell to [M1]. Jaipur has 695 to 1,299 gyms, depending on the count [M3][M4].
  Jodhpur has about 295 and Kota about 229 [M5].
- Nobody publishes how many Indian gyms use software. Vendors and guides say most small gyms still
  run on a register, Excel and WhatsApp groups [M6][M7]. So most of our buyers have no software today.
- Small gyms pay about ₹500 to ₹3,500 a month for software, or ₹12,000 to ₹15,000 a year for GGMS.

**What the money looks like (guess, prices without GST):**
- 10 paying gyms: we cover the ₹14,100 monthly cost only with founding-gym cash.
- 50 gyms in a year: about ₹42,000 a month plus about ₹6 lakh of founding cash. Profitable.
- 200 gyms in two years: about ₹2.4 lakh a month. Needs a second person and selling outside Jaipur.

**Our real edge:** every member gets a free, premium workout app (and watch app) in the gym's
name, and messages to members cost the gym ₹0. Indian rivals give members a thin app that shows
dates and fees. No Indian rival we found does both.

**Where we are weaker (be honest in every pitch):** no fingerprint or face machine link, no
automatic SMS or WhatsApp (free phone pop-ups do the job), no class booking, no iPhone app; UPI collection is built (F79) but waits for the payment account, no paying customers
and no reviews yet. We are one person; owners will ask "what if you disappear?"

**What would make us the clear best choice:** sell a service, not just software.
1. We set it up at your desk and move your records, free, in one visit.
2. Hindi WhatsApp support with a reply inside 1 hour, 9 am to 9 pm.
3. 60-day money back on yearly plans. No lock-in, your data leaves with you any time.
4. "App launch day": we come and help 50 members install the app.
5. Price locked for life for early gyms.

**Correction by Claude (6 Nov):** the first client gym is not live yet (F182 launch plan). Get it live first: the pitch "[Client gym] uses it" and the owner video both need it.

**How to get the first 10 Jaipur gyms:** walk in, area by area, with the demo on a phone. Use
the first client gym as the proof (a 60-second owner video). Pay equipment dealers, supplement
distributors and trainers a fixed reward for every gym that pays. Then for 50: the same in
Jodhpur and Kota, video-call demos, and a small Instagram test.

**First words to use:** "Run your gym's desk, and give every member a free workout app in your
gym's name. From ₹999 a month."

**What gyms pay today:**
- GGMS (Jaipur): ₹11,999 a year without member app, ₹14,999 with app [M9]; quoted to us as ₹15,000
  once + ₹5,000 a year (R-pricing).
- Cheap cloud tools: GymBoss ₹499 a month [M10]; Dgymbook ₹499; MyGymDesk from ₹899 [M11];
  AppToFit ₹1,099 [M12]; Gymate from ₹1,999 [M13]; G-Coding ₹1,500 [M14].
- Foreign tools (rare in small Indian gyms): Mindbody from about $79-159 a month plus 2.75% card

  a month [M17]; WellnessLiving from $99 [M12]. That is ₹7,000-17,000 a month: out of reach for
  a small Indian gym.

**Who we can really sell to (guess):** in Jaipur, the 400-600 value and mid gyms with 100+
members and an owner who has a smartphone. 10 gyms = 2% of them; 50 gyms = about 10%. Across
Rajasthan's five big cities, maybe 1,500-2,000 such gyms.

Notes:
- **Break-even = about 10 gyms paying monthly** (R-pricing). Founding gyms do not count toward it.
- **Founding gyms cost money later.** 25 gyms on Pro for life = about ₹37,500 a month we never
  bill. Fine as start-up cash; do not go past 25.
- **Losing gyms:** small business software in India loses customers often. I found no Indian gym
  number. If 2% of paying gyms leave each month (guess), scenario B ends year 2 at about 90, not 100.
- **GST:** if you register, either add 18% on top or keep 85% of each price.
- **Time to cash:** the 3-month free trial pushes every payment 3 months out. In scenario B, the
  first paying gym bills in month 4. Founding gyms and yearly pre-payment pull cash forward.

**What the evidence says:** Indian owners are told prices only after a sales call. Reviews are
few and mostly five stars, so they prove little. The real complaints we found: bugs after
updates, a learning curve, internet needed, extra costs for SMS and WhatsApp, and long contracts
on foreign tools. **To get real complaints, ask every gym we visit: "What annoys you about your
current software?"** and write it down. That is better data than any website.

### 3.2 Gaps they all share

1. **Members get almost nothing.** Rival member apps show the end date, fees and attendance. None
   gives a real workout tracker, and none gives a watch app.
2. **Messages cost extra.** SMS and WhatsApp reminders are paid per message (about ₹0.86 per
   WhatsApp offer, ₹0.115 per reminder, plus GST: R-pricing). Add-ons raise the real bill 20-40%.
3. **Hidden prices.** "Request a price" everywhere. Owners do not trust it.
4. **Set-up and data move are the owner's job.** Most tools give an Excel template, not a person.
5. **Tied in.** Buy-once desk software with paid yearly upkeep, or long contracts (Mindbody).
6. **They count fees, not members kept.** Reports exist, but nothing tells the owner "these 6
   members are about to leave, message them now".

### 3.3 Our real edges, and where we are weaker

| We are better | We are weaker |
|---|---|
| Free premium workout app for every member, in the gym's name and colours, with a watch app | **No fingerprint / face / turnstile link** (F103, waiting). GGMS has it. Many Jaipur gyms already own an eSSL machine. |
| Messages and offers to members' phones cost ₹0 | **No automatic SMS / WhatsApp** from the gym's number (only one-tap WhatsApp). Members without the app miss pop-ups. |
| Daily list of members who stopped coming, with a ready WhatsApp | **No class booking** (F139, waiting) |
| Works offline at the desk | **No UPI / card collection** (F79, waiting) |
| AI import of old records with undo; free data move | **No iPhone app** (F185, idea). Metro and premium gyms have many iPhone members. |
| Clear prices on the website; Hindi and English | **No salaries, no payroll** |
| Trainer workout plans, diet plans, shop, challenges in one plan | **No customers, no reviews, one-person company.** The biggest trust gap. |

### 3.4 Things to add or OFFER to be the clear best choice

These are mostly service and promises, not features. All cheap for us; all rare among rivals.

1. **"We set it up, you just say yes."** In Jaipur: one visit, we move the records (AI import),
   print the QR poster, train the desk person. Outside Jaipur: same on a video call within 48 hours.
2. **Hindi WhatsApp support, reply within 1 hour, 9 am to 9 pm, 7 days.** Put it in writing.
   It answers "what if you disappear?" better than any feature. Cost: your time (guess: 15-30
   minutes a day per 10 gyms).
3. **60-day money back on yearly plans. No lock-in. Your data in Excel any time.** Rivals tie
   owners in; we say "leave whenever you want". Risk is low: a gym that leaves in 60 days costs us
   only the trial.
4. **"App launch day."** We come on a busy evening with a standee and help members install the app.
   The free member app is our edge, and it only works if members install it. Target 50 installs on
   day 1 (guess).
5. **Price locked for life** for the first 50 gyms. Removes the fear of a price jump next year.
6. **Monthly "members kept" report on WhatsApp**: "This month MettleUp helped bring back 7 members
   = ₹10,500 in fees." Turns the bill into a clear saving. (The "Your growth" page already has the
   data, R-business-growth.)

**Build next, because they block sales:** the machine link (F103) and UPI collection (F79). Both
wait on the owner's hardware or payment account. The iPhone app matters for the 50-gym stage.

## 4. Marketing plan

### 4.1 Which channels work for Indian gym owners

What we found: owners in smaller cities rarely search Google for "gym software". They buy on
trust, from people they already know, and live on YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp [M8][M26]. One
Delhi gym-software company grew on field sales reps [M26]. Field sales can cost ₹15,000-30,000
per customer [M27] (one unverified source), so it must be the owner's own time at first, not paid staff.

| Channel | Cost | Why it works / watch out | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Walk-in visits (you)** | ₹0 + about ₹2,000 a month travel (guess) | Owners buy from a face. Go 11 am to 4 pm, when gyms are quiet. Show the demo on your phone. | First 10 |
| **First client gym as proof** | ₹0 | A 60-second video of the owner saying what changed. Ask him for 2 owner friends' numbers. | First 10, then always |
| **Gym equipment dealers** (e.g. Anup's Fitness, Impulse Fitness, Jerai in Jaipur [M28]) | ₹2,000 per gym that pays (guess) | They meet every NEW gym before it opens: the best moment to sell software. | 10 → 50 |
| **Supplement distributors** | ₹2,000 per gym that pays (guess) | They visit gyms every week. Offer: "the shop in the app sells your stock". | 10 → 50 |
| **Trainers** | ₹1,000 per gym that pays (guess) | Trainers move between gyms and talk to owners daily. The trainer plans feature helps them directly. | 10 → 50 |
| **Owner referral** | 1 month free for both (cost about ₹1,400) | Owners know other owners. Ask at month 2, when they are happy. | From gym 3 |
| **WhatsApp (personal, one by one)** | ₹0 with the free Business app | Never bulk-send to strangers: numbers get banned. Send after a visit or a referral. Paid API marketing messages cost about ₹0.86 each + GST (R-pricing). | Follow-up |
| **Instagram reels** | ₹0 organic; ads about ₹6-30 a click, local budgets ₹15,000-30,000 a month [M29] | Short reels: "how a member sees your gym's app". Test ads only at the 50-gym stage, ₹10,000 max. | 10 → 50 |
| **Google Business Profile** | ₹0 | So an owner who hears the name can find and trust us. Add photos and the first reviews. | Now |
| **Justdial** | Free listing; paid from about ₹100 a day (indicative) [M30] | Free listing yes; paid no for now. | Now (free) |
| **Local events** (bodybuilding shows, gym owner meets, "Mr Jaipur") | ₹5,000-20,000 a stall (guess) | Many owners in one room. One event a quarter, once there are 5 gyms to show. | 10 → 50 |

### 4.2 Four-week plan to the first paying gyms in Jaipur

**Week 1: get ready**
- Film the first client owner (60 seconds) and take 5 photos of his gym's app on members' phones.
- Make a printed one-page sheet (Hindi on one side, English on the other) and the QR demo card.
- Fill the Google Business Profile and the free Justdial listing.
- List 60 gyms in 3 nearby areas (for example Jagatpura, Malviya Nagar, Mansarovar), from Google
  Maps. Note which use GGMS or a machine.
- Ask the client owner for 3 owner friends to call.

**Week 2: first 20 visits**
- 4 visits a day, 5 days. Pitch, demo, leave the sheet, take the WhatsApp number.
- Send the WhatsApp message (below) the same evening.
- Goal: 5 owners who agree to a free set-up.

**Week 3: set-ups and partners**
- Set up the 5 trial gyms in person; run an "App launch day" in each.
- Meet 3 equipment dealers and 2 supplement distributors; agree the ₹2,000 reward in writing.
- 20 more visits.

**Week 4: ask for money**
- Offer each trial gym the founding deal (₹29,999, first 25 only, until 31 Mar 2027) or yearly
  Pro with 60-day money back.
- Goal at day 30: 3-5 founding gyms paid, 8-10 gyms on trial.
- Ask every gym on trial for one owner name.

**Then to 10, and on to 50 (months 2-12):**
- Keep 2-3 visit days a week until 10 paying gyms (about month 3-4).
- Month 4-6: dealer and trainer referrals; one local event; video-call demos for gyms that call.
- Month 6-9: two trips each to Jodhpur and Kota (about 520 gyms together [M5]); one partner dealer
  in each.
- Month 9-12: Instagram test (₹10,000); case studies with real numbers from 3 gyms.
- At about 30 paying gyms, hire a part-time local salesperson on commission (guess: ₹3,000 per paid gym).
