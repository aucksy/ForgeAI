**What a small gym pays:** ₹1,000-3,500 a month (₹10,000-36,000 a year) for up to 300 members,
says a vendor guide [S3]. One-time licences run ₹20,000-60,000 [S5]. Desk-only lifetime
licences go as low as ₹6,999 on IndiaMART [S13].

**Both shapes are normal in India.** Older desk software sells "buy once + yearly upkeep";
cloud tools sell monthly or yearly. Jaipur owners know the first shape best.

### The hidden costs owners already pay
- **WhatsApp offers cost money per message:** about ₹0.86 per marketing message, ₹0.115 per
  reminder, plus 18% GST [S14]. A 250-member gym sending 4 offers a month = about ₹1,000 a month.
  **Our messages to the member app cost the gym ₹0.** That alone nearly pays for Starter.
- **Fingerprint machine:** ₹4,650-7,000 (eSSL K30), ₹8,300-17,800 for face [S15]. The gym buys it.
- Software add-ons (SMS, WhatsApp, payment fees) raise the real bill 20-40% [S3].

### Jaipur gyms
- **How many:** 695 to 1,299 gyms, depending on the count [S16] [S17].
- **Fees members pay:** most Jaipur gyms charge ₹800-2,500 a month [S18]; a typical city gym
  ₹1,755 a month [S19]; Cult in Malviya Nagar ₹16,990 a year [S20]; Gold's Gym about ₹3,000 a month [S21].
- **Members per gym:** about 265 on average in India (46,500 gyms, 12.3 million members) [S22].
- **What that means (guess):** a 250-member Jaipur gym at ₹1,500 takes in about ₹3.75 lakh a month.
  Pro at ₹1,499 is 0.4% of that. **One member kept from leaving pays for the month.**

## 3. The plans in detail

Only features we have or have planned. "Coming" = not built yet.

### Starter: ₹999 a month · ₹9,990 a year
**Real value, nothing missing for the daily desk.**
- Members, plans, renewals, freeze, dues, part payments
- GST receipts and full tax invoices; expenses and profit; money reports and Excel
- Enquiries; staff logins (desk discount capped at 10%)
- Check-ins at the desk and by QR poster; offline desk without internet
- Today screen: ending today, in 1-3 / 4-7 / 8-15 days; daily list of members who stopped coming
- One-tap WhatsApp messages in English and Hindi (fees, endings, birthdays, receipts)
- Messages and offers to every member's phone, free (pop-ups through the app)
- Automatic reminders on members' phones (plan ending, birthday), switchable
- Free member app: workout tracker, "My membership", the gym's messages; MettleUp look with the gym's name
- Member ID cards; Excel and AI import of old records with undo; export all data any time

**Who:** 50-200 members, the owner sits at the desk, on paper, Excel or old desk software.
**Beats:** Dgymbook (₹499) and Easy Gym (₹799) on the free member app and free messages;
GGMS on no upfront ₹15,000. 3 years of Starter = ₹29,970, close to GGMS's ₹25,000, with a member app.

### Pro: ₹1,499 a month · ₹14,990 a year (the one we want most gyms on)
**Things a gym can live without, but which make members feel it is a premium gym.**
Everything in Starter, plus:
- **The gym's own logo and colours** in the member app, and the gym's icon on the home screen
- **Shop in the app:** members order supplements and gear, pay at the desk
- **Monthly challenge and leaderboard;** workout offers (10 workouts → 3 free days)
- **Trainer tools:** workout plans sent to phones with starting weights, diet plans, body measurements
- "How busy is the gym now" for members (in the next release)
- Coming: trainer sees members' progress; owner number tiles (analytics); bring-a-friend;
  members pay fees in the app (needs your payment account); entry gate link (needs the gym's machine)

**Who:** 150-600 members, has trainers, sells supplements, competes with Cult or Gold's nearby.
**Beats:** GGMS's app plan is ₹14,999 a year [S1]; Pro yearly is ₹14,990 for far more (own-brand
app, shop, challenges, workout tracker). Fitness Manager asks ₹75,000 a year for diet plans and
body checks [S6]; Pro has both at ₹14,990.

### Pro + AI: ₹2,499 a month · ₹24,990 a year (coming)
Everything in Pro, plus (all coming):
- AI helper for the owner: ask "who hasn't paid this month?", "busiest hour this week?"
- AI coach for members
**Who:** busy owners, 2+ branches. **Show it on the website now, sell it only when built.**

### Own it: ₹19,990 once, then ₹4,990 a year (on request only)
- Pro for one branch. The first year is in the ₹19,990. Upkeep from year 2 keeps updates,
  server and support. Skip upkeep and the gym keeps its data (Excel export); the app goes read-only.
- 3 years = ₹29,970. GGMS = ₹25,000 for less (no own-brand app, no shop, no challenges).
- **Say it on the phone, keep it off the website.** It is for owners who refuse monthly bills.
  On the website it would pull buyers away from Pro yearly (upkeep is only ₹416 a month to us).

### Founding gym: ₹29,999 once, Pro for life (first 25 gyms, until 31 Mar 2027)
- Same price as today. Cut from 50 to 25 gyms and add an end date: real scarcity, and fewer
  gyms we serve for free forever. The AI plan stays extra.
- Over 3 years it costs the same as "Own it", then nothing. Owners who plan to stay pick it.

### Extra branches
- Each extra branch at half price: Starter ₹499, Pro ₹749, Pro + AI ₹1,249 a month (decided A in #41).

## 4. Why the three prices work together (the anchor)

- **Pro sits in the middle and is the obvious pick.** From Starter to Pro is ₹500 more a month,
  but adds the gym's own app look, the shop, challenges and trainer tools. Most people pick the
  middle of three (the "compromise effect").
- **Pro + AI is the anchor.** At ₹2,499 it makes ₹1,499 look modest, even before it is built.
- **Pro yearly = ₹14,990, the same number as GGMS's app plan.** The owner can compare one to one.
- **Yearly = 10 months' price.** Same rule on every plan, easy to say: "pay yearly, 2 months free".
- **Starter is not a trap.** It runs the whole desk. Owners upgrade for pride and extra money
  (shop sales, challenges that keep members), not because Starter is broken.

3. **First year of Pro at the Starter price, ₹9,990,** if they pay yearly before the trial ends.

4. **Run both side by side for 2 weeks** before switching off GGMS (already in the launch plan).

## 6. Getting your money back

Costs: Claude ₹12,000 + server ₹2,100 = **₹14,100 a month.**

| Gyms paying... | Gyms needed to cover ₹14,100 |
|---|---|
| Starter monthly (₹999) | 15 |
| Pro monthly (₹1,499) | 10 |
| Pro yearly (₹14,990 = ₹1,249 a month) | 12 |
| Pro + AI monthly (₹2,499) | 6 |
| Own it, upkeep only (₹416 a month) | 34, so never the main plan |
| Mix (guess: 30% Starter, 60% Pro, 10% AI, about ₹1,450 average) | 10 |

- **10-15 paying gyms is about 1-2% of Jaipur's gyms.** Reachable in Jaipur alone.
- **Fastest cash:** 5 founding gyms = ₹1.5 lakh up front = about 10 months covered. The price:
  those 5 never pay again. Worth it once; that is why the cap is 25.
- **The 3-month trial delays cash by 3 months.** Most Indian tools give 7-30 days (#30).
  My pick: keep 3 months for the first 25 gyms, then drop it to 1 month. And: an owner who pays
  yearly on day 1 still keeps his free months (15 months for the price of 12). That pulls cash forward.
- **Cost per extra gym is small (guess):** workouts stay on phones, so our server cost per gym is
  tens of rupees a month. Almost every rupee after break-even is profit. AI will cost per question,
  which is why AI is a separate plan.
- **GST:** if you register (#32), add 18% on top, or the price falls to 85% for you. Decide before
  the first invoice.

## 8. What could stop a sale (be honest about these)

- **No fingerprint / face machine link yet.** GGMS has it. Gyms that use a machine today may wait.
  Answer: QR check-in now, machine link coming, "no price change when it arrives".
- **No automatic SMS or WhatsApp from the gym's number.** We send free app pop-ups instead, but
  only members who installed the app get them. App installs are the thing to push at every gym.
- **No class booking or staff salaries.** Rarely needed in small gyms; check on the gym visit.
- **Plans are not locked in the software yet.** Today every gym gets every feature. Moving the
  logo, shop, challenges and trainer tools into Pro needs a small build (the plan is already
  stored per gym). Gyms already on trial should keep everything until their trial ends.
