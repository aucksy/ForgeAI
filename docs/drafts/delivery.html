How MettleUp is delivered

MettleUp is online software. Nothing is shipped and there is no delivery charge. You use it in the MettleUp admin app and on app.mettleup.fit; your members use the free MettleUp app on their phones.

When you get access

  • Free trial: starts as soon as you sign up. No payment is needed.
  • After you pay by UPI or bank transfer (today): we turn your plan on in your MettleUp account once we see the payment. The screen shows your plan and the date it runs until.
  • After you pay by card or net banking (coming soon): your plan turns on at once, usually within seconds.
  • If your plan has not turned on after you paid, email contact@mettleup.fit with your gym name and the payment date. If your gym is signed in, the founder's phone number is inside the app.

How you pay

  • Today: you pay by UPI or bank transfer (NEFT or IMPS) from the "Pay MettleUp" screen in the app.
  • Coming soon: card and net banking, on the payment company's own secure page.
  • You get a receipt for every payment. MettleUp is not registered under GST; no GST is charged, so the receipt shows no GST.

How long access lasts

  • Monthly plan: one month from the day it starts. Yearly plan: one year. Founding gym offer: Pro for life, for one branch.
  • Before a monthly or yearly plan ends, we remind you 7, 3 and 1 days ahead. If you do not pay, you get 7 extra days with everything on. Then 30 days of basic mode: the desk keeps working for existing members (check-in, fee entry, member list), but you cannot add new members and the Pro extras are off. After that, MettleUp is read-only: you can look and export, and paying switches everything back on.
  • Your data is never deleted because a plan ends.

For refunds and cancelling, see refunds and cancellation.
