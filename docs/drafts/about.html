About MettleUp

MettleUp is software for running a gym in India. It is made and run by one person in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

What it does

  • For the gym owner and staff: a desk app for members, plans, renewals, dues, receipts and check-ins. The desk works even without internet.
  • For every member: a free workout app with the gym's name. Members see their own progress. Members never pay MettleUp.
  • Your member list can be brought in from any Excel sheet with AI. Set-up, and moving your data from your old software or Excel, is free.
