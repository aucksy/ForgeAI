MettleUp refunds and cancellation

This page is for gym owners who pay for MettleUp, the gym software. MettleUp is run by Aakash Pahuja, Jaipur, India. Members of a gym never pay MettleUp: the member app is free. Your membership fees and refunds at your gym are between you and your gym.

1. The free trial

  • Every new gym gets 3 months free. You pay nothing and no card is needed, so there is nothing to refund.
  • One free trial per gym phone number. [TO CONFIRM: same wording as the sign-up screen]

2. Money back on a yearly plan

  • 60-day money back on the first payment of a yearly plan. If you are not happy within 60 days of your first yearly payment, tell us and we refund that payment in full.
  • This applies to the first yearly payment only, not to later yearly renewals.
  • [TO CONFIRM: does 60-day money back cover the founding payment? Recommended: no]

3. Monthly plans

  • Monthly payments are not refunded for the current month.
  • You can stop any time. Your plan stays on until the end of the month you paid for, and then it does not renew.

4. Cancel any time, no lock-in

  • There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee.
  • When you cancel, your plan stays on until the paid date ends. After that it does not renew.
  • All your data goes to Excel in one tap, before or after you cancel.
  • Moving to a smaller plan starts when your current paid period ends. There is no money back for the days left, except under the 60-day money back above.
  • Moving to a bigger plan starts at once. You pay only the difference for the days left.

5. If you stop paying

  • On your plan's end date, you get 7 extra days with everything still on.
  • Then 30 days of basic mode: the desk keeps working for your existing members (check-in, fee entry, member list). You cannot add new members, and the Pro extras are off.
  • After that, MettleUp is read-only: you can look at everything and export it. Pay to switch everything back on.
  • Your data is never deleted.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the same way you paid (the same UPI, card or bank account).
  • The money reaches you within 5–7 working days (Monday to Saturday) after we approve the refund. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
  • MettleUp is not registered under GST; no GST is charged. Your receipt and any refund show the full amount, with no GST in it.

7. How to ask for a refund or to cancel

  • Email contact@mettleup.fit. If you are signed in, the founder's phone number is inside the app.
  • Tell us your gym name, the phone number you signed up with, and the date of the payment (or the receipt number).

Our promises to gyms

  • Free set-up, and we move your data from your old software or Excel.
  • 60-day money back on the first payment of a yearly plan (see section 2).
  • Free training for your trainers and desk staff, when you ask.
  • The same price for life for founding gyms (the first 25 gyms).
