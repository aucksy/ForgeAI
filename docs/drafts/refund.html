MettleUp refunds and cancellation

This page is for gym owners who pay for MettleUp, the gym software. Members of a gym never pay MettleUp: the member app is free. Your membership fees and refunds at your gym are between you and your gym.

1. The free trial

  • Every new gym gets 3 months free. No card is needed. You pay nothing during the free months, so there is nothing to refund.

2. We never charge you automatically

  • You pay only when you choose to pay. We never take money from your card or bank account on our own.
  • When your paid time ends, your plan does not renew by itself. To keep it, you pay again.
  • If we add automatic payments later, they stay off unless you switch them on. You can switch them off at any time.

3. Payments are not refunded

  • Once your paid plan is on, payments are not refunded. This is true for monthly plans, yearly plans and the founding gym payment.
  • A monthly plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. A yearly plan runs to the end of the year you paid for.
  • Please use the 3 free months to try MettleUp before you pay.
  • If you paid twice by mistake, or paid more than the price, we refund the extra amount.

4. AI credits

  • AI credits you buy are not refunded and cannot be changed into cash.
  • Credits you buy never expire.
  • Free credits for a month end on the last day of that month. Unused free credits do not move to the next month.
  • If a task fails after its credits are taken, the same credits come back to you automatically.

5. Cancel any time, no lock-in

  • There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee.
  • To stop, just do not pay again. Your plan stays on until the paid date ends. You can also tell us by email.
  • All your gym's data goes to Excel in one tap (Settings, in the admin app), before or after you stop.
  • Changing your plan: today, email us and we set it up for you. When online payments start: a smaller plan starts when your paid time ends, with no money back for the days left; a bigger plan starts at once, and you pay only the difference for the days left.

6. If you stop paying

  • We tell you in the admin app first. After that, we may switch off the paid features step by step.
  • When online payments start, it works like this: on your plan's end date you get 7 extra days with everything on. Then 30 days of basic mode: the desk keeps working for your existing members (check-in, fee entry, member list), but you cannot add new members and the Pro extras are off. After that, MettleUp is read-only: you can look at everything and export it. Pay again to switch everything back on.
  • We never delete your data because a payment is late. If you ask us to, we delete your gym's data within 30 days.

7. How a refund is paid

  • Refunds go back to the same way you paid (the same UPI, card or bank account).
  • The money reaches you within 5–7 working days (Monday to Saturday) after we approve the refund. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
  • MettleUp is not registered under GST. No GST is charged, so a refund has no GST in it.

8. How to cancel, or report a wrong payment

  • Email contact@mettleup.fit. More ways to reach us are on the contact page.
  • Tell us your gym name, the phone number you signed up with, and the date of the payment (or its reference number).

Founding gyms: "Pro for life" means for as long as MettleUp runs this service.
