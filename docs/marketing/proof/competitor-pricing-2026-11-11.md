# Indian gym software: top 5 by usage evidence, and their prices (11 Nov 2026)

Every number below was read live on 11 Nov 2026 unless marked otherwise. Play Store numbers came from the
Google Play India listing (hl=en_IN, gl=IN), read in the in-app browser. "L+" means lakh (1L+ = 100,000+).

**Caveat: no exact market share is public for Indian gym software.** No analyst report, government
data or audited figure exists. The ranking below rests on: (1) gym counts the vendors claim about
themselves (unverified, often global, not India-only); (2) Play Store installs and review counts of the
owner and member apps (independent, but some vendors sell a desktop or web product, so their installs undercount them);
(3) reviews on Indian software directories; (4) Meta ad activity (from RESEARCH-2026-11.md, 9 Nov).
Read it as "the biggest names we can see evidence for", not as a measured league table.

### Next in line (close behind, would make a top 7)
- **Okfit** (Aerobit Technologies): member app 10K+ installs, 101 reviews; Manager app 1K+. No gym count claimed. Public prices **excl. tax**: Lite 500, Basic 1,000, Essential 2,000, Growth 3,000 per month; yearly saves 20% (amounts not shown); 7-day trial, no card. International tiers USD 79 / 149 / 229.
- **MyGymDesk**: member app 1K+, business app 100+. Public prices, **excl. 18% GST**, no setup fee, 7-day trial with no card:
  Free (1.5% of collections, capped at 5,000 + GST a month; no branded app); Starter 699/mo or 6,999/yr (3 staff, 1 biometric);
  Growth 1,499/mo or 14,999/yr (10 staff, unlimited biometrics, class booking, CRM); Pro 2,499/mo or 24,999/yr
  (unlimited staff, payment gateway, branded app on yearly plans only); Enterprise 39,999/yr (website API, WhatsApp Business API, AI agent).
  *(Our 9 Nov note said 809-899; that is out of date.)*
- Other Indian owner apps with 10K+ installs: **Gymzee** (179 reviews), **GymOps** (291), **GGMS / Gayatri** (169),
  **Global Gym Software** (64), **Gymex** member app (31). **My Gym (Studycafe)** 5K+ (529 reviews), **OkGym** 5K+ (681).
  **GoGym4U** (3Click) shows 1L+ but mixes a consumer fitness app with the owner tool, so it is not comparable.
- **Gymshim**: claims 500+ gym owners; Play app 100+; demo only, no price. **GymManager (MDM)**: claims 400+ gyms in India, custom price, 14-day trial.
- **Not material in India**: Wellyx (no INR pricing; USD 99 / 199 / 299), Glofox (staff app 10K+ globally), Mindbody (Business app 5L+ globally),
  Zenoti (salons and spas first). VIOFIT (10+ installs), Gyms Cloud (1+) and Akton (50-100) are tiny on Play.

## What it means for MettleUp's pricing
- The mass market is cheap: the two most installed owner apps cost **about 2,200-5,600 a year** (GymBook 2,199 + GST; Dgymbook 5,592 incl. GST).
  The advertised entry point everywhere sits at **500-800 a month**. Anything above roughly 1,000 a month needs a reason the owner can see.
- Premium exists only for chains: FitnessForce runs **1,500-5,000 a month excl. GST** for multi-branch clubs, sold by demo.
- Two of the top five (YDL, Easy Gym) and FitnessForce hide prices behind a form. Public prices plus a no-card trial still set us apart from them,
  but not from GymBook, Dgymbook, Okfit or MyGymDesk, which already publish theirs.
