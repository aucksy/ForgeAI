Our edge: a real workout tracker in the gym's name (a target on every set, records, a watch app), free set-up
and data move, published prices.

## Member app (members)
Starts after Play production access, only through live gyms. App QR check-in from day 1; the trainer installs
it on visit 1. Hooks: know what to lift today; never forget last week's weight; your trainer's plan (Pro);
watch yourself get stronger; your gym's leaderboard (Pro); free for members of MettleUp gyms.
No public paid test until F56 (paid plan for solo users) is settled. The member site's "Recommend MettleUp to
your gym" link (F184) turns solo users into gym leads. Watch: side point (advanced watches, Apple included,
are about 13% of smartwatches sold in India). Metric: app check-ins per member per week.

one paying gym costs about Rs8,000-33,000 = 5-33 months of fees. The first test buys learning, not customers.
