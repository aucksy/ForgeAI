# MettleUp UAT + UX review, 30 Oct 2026

No code was changed. Nothing was saved, sent, imported, deleted, paid or approved.

## What was covered, and what was not
- **Member app:** NOT run here (no Android tools, by rule). Judged from the cloud phone test pictures
  (360 dp, large text, 1080x2400) of qa-device run 40999691263 (commit f83c428, owner phone) and
  qa-journeys run 41012405375 (commit 986d19e), plus the screen code in `apps/mobile/src`.
  About 25 of the roughly 300 pictures were read closely. The rest were not opened. Treat this as a
  sample, not a full audit.
- **Desk site (app.mettleup.fit):** the owner signed in to the built-in browser. Every main tab and More
  screen was read at 1280 px and checked at 390 px (list under "Desk screens walked" below). "Start demo"
  and "Create my gym" make a new gym, so they were not pressed.
- **Banned member words ("volume", "PR", "session"):** none found on the pictures read. A code search of
  member screen text (titles, labels, captions, button words) found none either. Internal names such as
  `/session/` routes and `volume.ts` are never shown. Not proven for every screen.

## Table B: UX ideas
| id | desk or app | screen | idea (one sentence) | what the user gains | effort | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-01 | app | All screens | Move the chat button into the top bar (the active workout already has one there), or hide it while scrolling. | Nothing is covered. Buttons and list arrows are always tappable. | small | A-01 shots |
| B-02 | app | Finish summary | Lead with the good news ("Push Day A done", records) and show the total weight below it. Only compare with a past workout of similar size. | The member feels the win first, and never reads "lighter" after a short workout. | small | shots/app-15-finish-summary.png |
