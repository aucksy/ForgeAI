MettleUp shipping and delivery

MettleUp is online software. Nothing is shipped and there is no delivery charge. You use it in the MettleUp admin app and on app.mettleup.fit. Your members use the free MettleUp app on their phones.

When you get access

  • Free trial: starts as soon as you sign up. No payment is needed.
  • After you pay by UPI or bank transfer (today): you send us the payment reference from the "Pay MettleUp" screen. We check it and turn your plan on. The screen then shows your plan and the date it runs until.
  • After you pay by card or net banking (coming soon): your plan turns on at once, usually within seconds.
  • Founding gym offer: email us to pay for it. It cannot be paid from the "Pay MettleUp" screen.
  • If your plan has not turned on after you paid, email contact@mettleup.fit with your gym name and the payment date. More ways to reach us are on the contact page.

How you pay

  • Today: by UPI or bank transfer (NEFT or IMPS), from the "Pay MettleUp" screen in the admin app (Settings).
  • Coming soon: card and net banking, on the payment company's own secure page.
  • Today, the "Pay MettleUp" screen lists each payment you sent and whether we confirmed it. When online payments start, you also get a receipt for every payment.
  • MettleUp is not registered under GST. No GST is charged.

How long access lasts

  • Monthly plan: one month from the day it starts. Yearly plan: one year. Founding gym offer: Pro for one branch, for as long as MettleUp runs this service.
  • When online payments start, we will remind you in the admin app 7, 3 and 1 days before a monthly or yearly plan ends.
  • If you do not pay, we tell you in the admin app first. After that, we may switch off the paid features step by step. See refunds and cancellation, section 6.
  • We never delete your data because a payment is late. If you ask us to, we delete your gym's data within 30 days.

For refunds and cancelling, see refunds and cancellation.
