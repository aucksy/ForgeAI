Why today felt harder: where the line shows

R-344 (owner, 5 Nov 2026). When a workout is lighter than last time, MettleUp adds one calm line: last night's sleep when it was under 6 hours, otherwise, now and then, a protein tip. Three places it could show. A and C are built; B is not.

A. Workout complete, under the big card Picked

The member has just seen "Lighter than last time". The reason sits right under it, at the one moment they wonder why. It shows once, with no heading and no button, so it never nags.

B. During the workout, after a lighter set Not built

Shown between sets when a set falls short. Rejected: mid-workout the member needs the next weight, not a reason to feel tired. One short set is not a short day; the line would appear on most workouts and turn into noise.

C. Settings, Health apps: the switch Picked

Off until the member turns it on. Turning it on opens Health Connect's own screen to allow "Sleep". The caption says in one line that the sleep stays on the phone. The protein tips need no switch.

Rules: the line shows only when the big card says "Lighter than last time" (never on an easy week). Sleep under 6 hours comes first. Otherwise a protein tip shows at most once every 3 days, and the 3 tips take turns. A tip never states what the member ate, because there is no food log yet. Sleep is read on the phone for this one line and is never stored, backed up, or sent to MettleUp, the gym or any AI. No Health Connect, sleep not allowed, or no clear night means the sleep line does not show; the app never guesses.
