H1.4 | gap-1 | A should-read with no read = finding
H6.1 | gap-6 | For every field a feature depends on (birthday, phone, check-in phone, app link, plan dates, trainer, batch): how many Crispy Curves rows have it filled?
H6.2 | gap-6 | Is there a screen that asks for it at the right moment?
H6.3 | gap-6 | A feature that depends on a field most rows lack = finding
H6.4 | gap-6 | For every input box (mobile number, name, date): feed it what a phone keyboard autofill or a paste really puts in (country code, spaces, dashes, digits in other scripts) and check that the length limit and the cleaning step together keep a valid value
H6.5 | gap-6 | a box that cuts or refuses a real autofill = finding
