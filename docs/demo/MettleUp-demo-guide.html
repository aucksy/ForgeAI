MettleUp — demo guide

Gym desk website: app.mettleup.fit (laptop or phone)  ·  Member app: MettleUp on Android.  Arrows (→) show what to tap, in order.

Before the client arrives
Desk: sign in at app.mettleup.fit and open Today.
Phone: open MettleUp. On a fresh phone, tap Load demo data instead on the welcome screen so charts and records are full.
Print the gym QR poster: Visits → Check-in QR → Print poster.

Demo order (about 20 minutes)

  1. The owner's morning. Today: money owed, renewals due, members who stopped coming, new members, busy hours.
  2. Plans ending soon. Tap a tile, e.g. Ending in 1-3 days → WhatsApp all. One tap per member, the message is already written.
  3. A walk-in enquiry. Today → New enquiry → save → later Add as member.
  4. Join and pay. Today → New member → Take photo → Sell membership → Collect payment → receipt → Send on WhatsApp.
  5. Member card. On the member's page → Member card → Print or Send on WhatsApp.
  6. Member joins the app. Desk: Settings → Member app → Send link on WhatsApp. Phone: Gym → Join your gym → code → Next.
  7. Check in with the poster. Phone: Gym → Check in → scan. Desk: Visits shows the visit.
  8. Log a workout. Phone: Workout → Start empty workout → Add exercise → Add set. Show the rest timer and the “kg to beat” target → Finish workout → Picture to share.
  9. Progress. Phone: Progress → Records, Charts, Body, Reports.
  10. Gym sends a plan. Desk: member → Workout plans → Send a plan. Phone: it appears on the Workout tab, marked “From ‹gym›”.
  11. Keep members coming. Desk: Challenges → New challenge. Phone: Gym → Leaderboard.
  12. The money picture. Desk: Money and Expenses (Collected, Expenses, Profit) → Download Excel file.
  13. Moving from Excel or another app. Desk: Members → Import from Excel → Choose file. AI matches the columns by itself.
  14. Make it theirs. Desk: Branding → Upload logo → Colour → Save. The member app shows the gym's logo.
Points to say
• The desk works on a laptop or a phone, and keeps working when the internet drops.
• Each staff member gets their own sign-in code and PIN.
• Members get a full workout app for free, with the gym's name on it. That is why they keep coming back.
