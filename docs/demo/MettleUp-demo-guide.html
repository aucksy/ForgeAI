MettleUp — demo guide

Gym desk website: app.mettleup.fit (laptop or phone)  ·  Member app: MettleUp on Android. Arrows (→) show what to tap, in order. On a phone, the desk keeps Shop, Messages & offers and the rest under More.

For the gym (desk website)

FeatureWhere to find it
Only in MettleUp — GGMS does not list these. Show them first.
1Gym shopMembers order protein, gear and drinks on the phone. Pay and collect at the desk.Shop → Turn on the shop · Products → + Add product · Orders → Mark ready → Collected (paid)
2Messages and offers to members' phonesFestival offer, gym closed, new timings and more. Pop-up on the phone, no SMS cost. See who read it.Messages & offers → Messages → Offers → Festival offer → Who gets it → Send in the app
3Workout offerTrain enough, get free days. Builds the habit, so members renew.Messages & offers → Workout offer → Start offer
4Challenges and leaderboardWorkouts or kg lifted; free days as the prize.Challenges → New challenge → Start challenge
5Gym logo and colours in the member appThe app carries the gym's name, logo and colour.Branding → Upload logo → Save
6Requests from the appNEWMembers ask to pause or renew; approve with one tap.Today → Requests from the app → Approve / Decline
7Import from any Excel, with AIAI matches the columns. One tap undoes an import.Members → Import from Excel → Choose file · Past imports → Undo
Everything else
8Today screenThe numbers an owner needs each morning.Today
9Members and plansAdd a member, sell a plan.Members → + Add member → Sell membership → Plan → Confirm
10Payments and receiptsCollect, print or WhatsApp a receipt; GST lines print by themselves.Member → Collect payment → Save payment · GSTIN: Settings → Gym details
11DuesEveryone who owes money, in one list.Money → Dues → Collect
12Plans ending soon + WhatsAppToday, 1-3, 4-7, 8-15 days; one tap each.Today → Ending in 1-3 days → WhatsApp all

14QR check-in and visitsMembers scan a poster; no machine needed.Visits → Check-in QR → Print poster · Visits → Register
15Members losing the habitEveryone who has not come for 10 days.Visits → Losing the habit
16EnquiriesWalk-ins and calls, with follow-up dates.Enquiries → + Add enquiry → Save enquiry → Add as member
17Expenses and profitCollected, expenses and profit each month.Expenses → + Add expense
18Workout and diet plansSend a plan straight to the member's phone.Member → Workout plans → Send a plan · Member → Diet plan
19Staff sign-ins and PINEach person as Staff or Owner.Settings → Staff & sign-in → Add a person → Make sign-in code
20Excel downloadsReceipts, dues, growth, or all gym data.Money → Collections → Download Excel file · Settings → Your data

Also live: personal training plans · member ID card · desk photo · body measurements · gym timings · more than one branch.

For the member (MettleUp app)

Tabs at the bottom: Workout, Progress, Gym, Profile. The Gym tab has 3 parts at the top: Shop (when the gym sells things), My gym, Leaderboard.

FeatureWhere to find it
Only in MettleUp — GGMS does not list these. Show them first.
