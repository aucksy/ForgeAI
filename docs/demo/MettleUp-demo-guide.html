Before the client arrives
Desk: sign in at app.mettleup.fit with a real gym (the demo gym hides Shop, Messages and Challenges). The gym must be online: Settings → Put your gym online.
Shop: Shop → Turn on the shop, and give 2 products a photo. Start one Workout offer and one Challenge.
Phone: MettleUp 0.62.0, joined to that gym (Gym → Join your gym). Check in once with the poster so “My gym” shows the plan.
Print the gym QR poster: Visits → Check-in QR → Print poster.

Demo order (about 25 minutes)

  1. The shop. Phone: Gym → Shop → order a protein → Place order. Desk: Shop → Orders shows it → Mark ready → the phone says “Collect it at the desk” → Collected (paid).
  2. A festival offer to every member. Desk: Messages & offers → Messages → Offers → Festival offer → Send in the app. The phone gets the pop-up. Desk shows who read it.
  3. The workout offer. Phone: Gym → My gym → Join the offer. Show “C of N workouts”. Desk: the member is in the offer list.
  4. The owner's morning. Desk: Today: money owed, renewals due, members losing the habit, shop orders, requests from the app.
  5. Plans ending soon. Ending in 1-3 days → WhatsApp all. One tap per member, the message is already written.
  6. Join and pay. Today → New member → Take photo → Sell membership → Collect payment → receipt → Send on WhatsApp.
  7. Check in with the poster. Phone: Gym → My gym → Check in → scan. Desk: Visits shows the visit.
  8. Log a workout. Phone: Workout → Start empty workout → Add exercise → Add set. Show the rest timer and the kg to beat → Finish workout → Picture.
  9. Challenge and leaderboard. Phone: Gym → Leaderboard. Desk: Challenges.
  10. Pause from the app. Phone: Gym → My gym → Pause my plan → Send to the desk. Desk: Today → Requests from the app → Approve.
  11. Gym sends a plan. Desk: member → Workout plans → Send a plan. Phone: it shows on the Workout tab.
  12. Their logo. Desk: Branding → Upload logo → Save. The phone shows the gym's logo.
  13. Moving from GGMS. Desk: Members → Import from Excel → Choose file. AI matches the columns.
  14. The money picture. Desk: Money and Expenses → Download Excel file.
Points to say
• Shop, workout offers and messages to phones: the gym earns more, and members keep coming.
• Members get a full workout app for free, with the gym's name on it.
• The desk works on a laptop or a phone. Each staff member gets their own sign-in code and PIN.
