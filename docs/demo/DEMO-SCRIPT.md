# 10-minute demo at a gym (R-540)

Open **app.mettleup.fit/demo** on a laptop or phone. It is a made-up gym; nothing you tap is saved, and a reload
starts it over. To show what the front desk sees, open **app.mettleup.fit/demo?as=desk**.

| # | Open | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Today** | "This is your morning in one screen: money in today, whose plan ends this week, who owes you, and what members asked for." |
| 2 | Today: **Plans ending** tile | "Eight plans end this week. Remind each person from here; two others already renewed early this morning. One member has a birthday today, so the app wishes them for you." |
| 3 | **Money** | "Every rupee by cash or UPI, with a receipt number, so the desk can't lose a payment." |
| 4 | Members: **Expired** filter | "These are members you've lost. Message them from here to bring them back." |
| 5 | **Enquiries** | "Every walk-in and phone call gets a follow-up date. Here's who to call today, and how many joined." |
| 6 | Enquiries: a **friend** referral | "Members bring friends from the app. When the friend joins, the member gets 7 free days on their plan." |
| 7 | Enquiries: **joined online** | "People can sign up from your web page and pay at the desk. You just confirm." |
| 8 | **Messages & offers** | "Send an offer or a 'gym closed' notice to every member's phone. No SMS cost, and you see who read it." |
| 9 | **Shop** → Orders | "Members order protein and shakers in the app. The desk marks it ready, the member collects." |
| 10 | **Training progress** | "Your trainers see who's getting stronger and who's dropping off, before they stop coming." |
| 11 | A member in Training progress | "This is their last 12 weeks, lift by lift. The member sees their own progress in the app too." |
| 12 | **Member questions** / **Member messages** | "Members ask a trainer or the gym from the app. Complaints come only to you, the owner." |
| 13 | **Diet plans** / **Workout plans** | "Give a ready-made Indian diet or a routine to one member or a whole plan, and it shows in their app." |
| 14 | **Trainers** | "Each trainer's hours and this week's personal training sessions, in one place." |
| 15 | **Challenges** | "A monthly challenge with a leaderboard in the app. Everyone who reaches the goal wins free days." |
| 16 | **Your growth** | "Income against rent, salaries and bills, month by month. You can see if the gym is growing." |
| 17 | **Staff & sign-in** | "Your desk and trainers each sign in with their own number. Trainers see only their members." |

Close with: "Your own gym starts with your members and plans. Your desk can be using it today."

Keep away from in a demo: the AI Assistant (it is not in the demo; real use spends AI credits) and Branding, entry
machines and importing (they need a real gym, and the demo says so on screen).
