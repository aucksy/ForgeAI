# R-630: An under-18 stop removes saved data, and every member has a dated age and consent record

Lane: Full. Card: docs/changes/R-630.json. Version 3 (27 Nov 2026), after two fresh review rounds (round 1:
3 reviewers, 30 findings; round 2: 1 critic, 8 findings; all answered below). Written for a reader with NO other context: see the glossary at the end.

Family (the cause): consent, age and terms are not recorded or enforced in one place. Master family M22 in
docs/system-map/FAMILIES.md (sub-families D-02, A-20, D-65; the 11 audit finding ids are on the card).

## 1. The problem, in the user's words
MettleUp's privacy notice (privacy.html section 9, "Under 18") promises: "MettleUp is only for people aged 18 or
over. If we learn that we hold data of someone under 18, we delete it." The terms say "You must be 18 or older".
Today the product does not keep that promise:
- A member who types an age under 18 stops new backups, but what is already saved on MettleUp's server stays.
  The gym summary keeps uploading. The Settings backup line still says workouts are "saved automatically". (S1)
- Members who typed an age under 18 before 25 Nov 2026 were backed up, and nothing will ever delete that copy.
- The age is a fixed number. A member who typed 17 stays 17 forever.
- The phone keeps the "I'm 18 or older, and I agree" tick only on the phone (version '2026-11-10'). The website
  self-join already records its own tick in the gym's records (wording version 2026-11-14). The app's gym join
  and sign-ins record nothing on the server. The terms were last changed 18 Nov 2026 and the privacy notice
  15 Nov 2026; members were never asked again.
- After the first tick, the app has no way to open the privacy notice or the terms again.
- The gym desk can add a child as a member, with photo, birthday and address.
- Trainer summary, entry-machine sharing and gym pop-ups turn on by themselves at joining. The privacy notice
  gives "legitimate interest" as the reason. India's data law (DPDP Act 2023) has no such reason.
- Gym owners: the gym terms changed on 14 Nov 2026, but the gym terms version was not raised, so owners were not
  asked again. MettleUp's HQ app cannot see which version each gym accepted.
- The gym's assistant and Messages page put members on offer lists with no way to leave out a member who asked
  not to get offers.

## 3. What users see after the change

### 3a. Member sets an age under 18 (onboarding, or Settings → Profile)
- A sheet opens before the age is saved: "MettleUp is for people 18 and older. If you are under 18, MettleUp
  deletes everything it keeps about you on its server, and your workouts stay only on this phone." Buttons:
  "Delete and continue" and "Cancel".
- Cancel: the age is NOT saved; the old age stays; nothing is deleted. (So "the app has seen an age under 18"
  always means the member confirmed it.)
- Delete and continue: the age is saved. The phone stops every upload to MettleUp's server AND to the gym (gym
  summary, sharing, pop-up sign-up). Two deletes run (section 7 lists what each removes):
  (1) if the member is in a gym, a delete keyed on the gym member key removes the trainer summary, the sharing
  rows and the pop-up sign-up (this needs no MettleUp sign-in); (2) if signed in, the under-18 account delete.
  The backup line shows, only AFTER the server confirms: "Deleted. MettleUp no longer keeps your workouts or
  account. Your gym still keeps its own member record of you. Your workouts are only on this phone."
- No internet: the line says "Waiting for internet to delete your saved copy." The delete is kept as a pending
  job and runs when the phone is online.
- Not signed in, but this phone remembers an account that saved from it (cloud/account.ts keeps it), whether the
  session expired or the member signed out: the line says "Sign in once to delete your saved copy." That
  sign-in is allowed for under 18 only to run the delete; it then signs out at once. Settings also has "Delete
  my saved copy" for anyone not signed in.

### 3b. Member already under 18 when this update installs
- On the first open after the update, the same sheet from 3a shows, because ages typed before 25 Nov 2026
  changed nothing and may be wrong. Cancel opens the age to fix it; nothing is deleted. "Delete and continue"
  runs the same deletes as 3a. Until the member answers, nothing uploads.

### 3c. Age grows
- The phone stores the age AND the day it was typed. Age now = typed age + full years since that day. A member
  counts as 18 only when that sum reaches 18 (never early; at most a year late, which is the safe side).
- Old phones: the day typed is unknown, so it is set to the day of the update (safe side again).
- When the age reaches 18, nothing turns on by itself. The backup card shows its normal "Keep my workouts safe"
  button, which asks for the tick first, as for any adult.

### 3d. The tick and the re-ask
- One current member terms version for every path: '2026-11-18' (the newest of the terms, 18 Nov, and the
  privacy notice, 15 Nov). The website self-join, the app gym join, Google sign-in and phone sign-in all send it.
- A member whose kept tick is older (every member today) sees a sheet once, on the next open: "Our terms and
  privacy notice changed on 18 Nov 2026" with a "What changed" list, the links, the same tick box, and the
  sharing switches (3e). Until ticked, the phone sends the gym no summary and none of the three optional
  sharings; check-ins, bookings, payments and messages keep working; workouts on the phone work; the desk keeps
  working. The sheet comes back on each open until ticked.
- Where the tick is kept: on the phone, AND on the server: in the gym's member record for a gym member (keyed on
  the gym member id, because most gym members have no MettleUp sign-in), and on the MettleUp account when signed
  in. A second phone of the same signed-in account reads the account's tick and does not ask again. A gym member
  with no sign-in who moves phone re-joins with the gym code anyway, and ticks then.
- The phone keeps the tick "pending" until the server confirms it saved it, and resends it.

### 3e. Sharing at joining (HIM question 2; his direction 27 Nov 2026)
- One box, empty at first: "Share with my gym: a summary of my workouts for my trainers, the machines I use, and
  messages from my gym". Ticking it turns on all three. The member can join without it.
- Beside it, an "i" that visibly changes when open (filled, slightly larger). Open, it explains each one with the
  member's benefit (his words, 27 Nov 2026): trainers see when you stop progressing and can guide you; machine use
  shows when the gym and each machine are busy, so you can pick a quiet time (nobody sees your name); gym messages
  bring important updates (opening hours, holidays, your plan ending). Then: "You can change each one later in
  Settings, under Your gym."
- Under the box, "Choose one by one" opens the three switches.
- Existing members see the same box once on the re-ask sheet; their current switches stay until they answer.
- Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Qp9Q3okCP8dLMmr7jmwRR4 (waiting for his yes).

### 3f. Settings → About
- New rows "Privacy notice" and "Terms" open https://mettleup.fit/privacy.html and /terms.html.

### 3g. Desk adds a member (HIM question 1; this step waits for his answer)
- Recommended: a birthday under 18 shows a required tick "A parent or guardian agreed". The desk records who
  ticked and when. That member can never be added to MettleUp's own cloud features or offer lists.

### 3h. "No offers"
- A switch on the member profile in the desk app. It is kept on the member AND on their phone number, and stays
  after the member leaves and rejoins or appears as an enquiry. It removes them from every offer audience: the
  Messages page queue, the assistant's WhatsApp batches, in-app offer pop-ups, and every "reaches N people"
  count. It does NOT stop service messages the member needs (payment due, plan ending, booking changes).

### 3i. Gym owners and HQ
- The gym terms version is raised to '2026-11-14'. Each owner is asked once at sign-in (the existing re-ask),
  and the acceptance keeps every field the data agreement promises (gym, who ticked, when, which version, how,
  the scrambled address). If HIM question 1 adds a gym duty for under-18 records, that goes into the SAME raise,
  so owners are asked once.
- HQ: each gym's accepted version and date; a list of gyms still on an older version.

### Must NOT see
- An under-18 member told their workouts are "saved automatically", or told "Deleted" before the server confirmed.
- Any MettleUp server data of a member who confirmed an age under 18, after the delete has run.
- Any new upload for that account from any phone with this version or later (the deleted account refuses it,
  and the server refuses a new sign-in that sends an under-18 age). An app from before 25 Nov 2026 on a second
  phone cannot be stopped: it has no age check; this is accepted and noted.
- A gym owner, trainer or desk login removed by any delete in this card.
- A gym summary or sharing upload from an under-18 phone.
- The tick sheet again after ticking the current version (same phone, or another phone of the same account).
- A member with "No offers" on any offer list, pop-up or count.
- A member over 18 blocked by an old age number for more than a year.
- Sharing running before its switch is on (if HIM 2 is "off by default").
- A child added at the desk on any offer list or cloud feature.

## 4. Questions
[F] questions, with evidence:
- Age: phone SQLite `user_profile.age INTEGER NOT NULL` (apps/mobile/src/db/schema.ts:13); rule
  `backupAllowedForAge` (apps/mobile/src/cloud/phoneAccount.ts:20-23). Read also by the copy format
  (pack.ts:451-469, copyLocal.ts:577), the AI coach (ai/system.ts:23), the file backup (backupRestore.ts:402),
  the profile editor (ProfileCard.tsx:112, profileAutosave.ts:69).
- The stop: `profileUnder18()` (copySync.ts:128-137) skips workout uploads; accountStore.ts:139-145 refuses
  Google and phone sign-in for under 18. The gym summary upload (outbox.ts:26-83) has no check.
- Delete: `member_account_delete()` (supabase 0032) deletes `member_items`, and the whole account when the person
  has no gym role and no gym. It is NOT widened: a Google sign-in in the member app can join onto a gym owner's
  or trainer's existing login (0032:58-62, 209-213, 222), so a wider delete would remove desk logins. This card
  adds two new calls instead, in migration 0101 (the one owner of this change):
  `member_under18_delete()`: signed-in caller; refused unless the caller has no owner, trainer or desk role;
  deletes `member_items`, `member_summary`, the account consent fields, the account's pop-up sign-ups, and the
  account (auth user and profile). `gym_member_under18_delete(member key)`: deletes, for that gym member, the
  trainer summary (0050_trainer_view.sql:70-71, keyed on the gym member), the sharing rows and the pop-up sign-up.
  The existing `member_account_delete` also clears the new account consent fields.
- Server functions refuse callers who are not signed in (0032:132, 186, 199, 220): hence the sign-in-to-delete
  path in 3a.
- The tick: apps/mobile/src/shop/joinTerms.ts:30 `TERMS_AGREED_VERSION = '2026-11-10'`, kept in phone `meta`.
- Gym join in the app: gymApi.ts:86-92 sends only the gym code, name and join id; the gym member key is the
  member's only identity (types.ts:90-96). Website self-join already requires and stores a tick
  (0089_trial_alert_words.sql:48, 83, 93).
- Gym terms: 0033 is live (0067 applied it on 10 Nov 2026 and recorded an acceptance; 0033's "NOT APPLIED" header
  is stale and is corrected in this card). `_terms_versions()` is the list of gym versions.
- Watch: does not read the age (searched apps/mobile/android/wear/src/main: nothing).
- Old apps: join function changes REPLACE the existing versions with optional new inputs, so old apps' calls
  still match. Missing new inputs (an old app) mean: the three optional sharings OFF and no tick recorded.
  gymApi.ts:79-83's fallback (resend without the new inputs when the server is older) is KEPT so joins never
  break before 0101 is live; in that case the phone keeps the tick "pending" and resends it later. Release rule:
  0101 goes live before the app version that needs it is released.

## 7. Copies of the data
- Age and the day it was typed: phone only (user_profile), and inside the cloud copy as a new trailing field
  (old phones ignore it and keep reading the age). Erased by "Erase all data".
- Tick: phone `meta` (with a pending flag); the gym's member record (version, when, how); the MettleUp account
  (version, when, how). Deleted with the account or the gym membership.
- The under-18 deletes remove: for the account, `member_items`, `member_summary`, the account consent fields,
  pop-up sign-ups and the account itself (never an owner, trainer or desk login); for the gym member, the trainer
  summary, sharing rows and pop-up sign-up. They do not remove the gym's own member record (name, phone, photo:
  the gym's data under the data agreement); the "Deleted" line says so; HIM 1 decides the wider rule.
  Nightly backups age out within 7 days.
- "No offers": the gym's member record and a per-gym phone-number list; deleted when the gym is deleted.
- Gym terms acceptance: existing append-only table; one new version line.

## 8. Risks and the pre-mortem
It failed one month after launch because:
- An adult lost their saved copy by a typo (1 for 21): guard = the sheet before saving, with Cancel keeping the
  old age; the delete runs only after "Delete and continue".
- An old app version kept uploading for a deleted account: guard = the account is gone, so its calls fail.
- The tick never reached the server, but the phone thought it had: guard = pending until confirmed; no silent
  fallback.
- Every member was nagged by the re-ask and some stopped using the gym features: guard = one sheet, one tap; the
  desk still works; measured after release (sheet shown vs ticked).
- A 17-year-old started uploading early: guard = age grows only from the day typed; never early.
- The iPhone build broke: guard = no Android-only code; the iPhone check list runs.
