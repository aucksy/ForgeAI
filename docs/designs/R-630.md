# R-630: An under-18 stop removes saved data, and every member has a dated age and consent record

Lane: Full. Card: docs/changes/R-630.json. Family (the cause): consent, age and terms are not recorded or
enforced in one place (master family M22; sub-families D-02, A-20, D-65; 11 audit findings).
Written for a reader with NO other context.

## 1. The problem, in the user's words
MettleUp's privacy notice says (section 9 "Under 18"): "MettleUp is only for people aged 18 or over. If we learn
that we hold data of someone under 18, we delete it." Today the product does not keep that promise:
- A member who types an age under 18 stops new backups, but everything already saved on MettleUp's server stays
  there. The backup line in Settings still says the workouts are "saved automatically". (S1)
- The age is stored as a fixed number. A member who typed 17 is treated as 17 forever, even after turning 18.
- The "I'm 18 or older, and I agree" tick is kept only on the phone. When a member joins a gym, the server keeps
  no record that they ticked it, which terms version, or when.
- Members who joined before the tick existed are never asked. Members are not asked again when the terms change.
- After the first tick, the app has no way to open the privacy notice or terms again.
- The gym admin panel (the desk) can add a child as a member with photo, birthday and address.
- Some sharing with the gym (trainer and entry-machine sharing) starts by itself at joining. Its legal basis
  ("legitimate interest") does not exist in India's data law (DPDP Act 2023).
- Gym owners: the gym terms changed on 14 Nov 2026 but owners were not asked to accept again, and MettleUp HQ
  cannot see which terms version each gym accepted.
- The gym's assistant puts every member without the app on WhatsApp offer lists, with no way to leave out a
  member who asked not to be messaged.
Who: every member and every gym. How often: every join, every sign-in, every age edit.

## 3. What users see after the change
- Member, age under 18 (set at onboarding or in Settings): sees "MettleUp saves nothing on its server for people
  under 18. Your workouts stay on this phone." Their saved server copy is deleted once, at that moment; the line
  says so ("Your saved copy on MettleUp's server was deleted.").
- Member who later turns 18 (by birth year): the backup can be turned on; they are asked for the tick first.
- Member already in a gym, with no tick for the current terms: asked once, on next open, with the same tick box
  and words as at joining. Until ticked, gym features that send data to the gym wait; workouts on the phone work.
- Member joining a gym (invite link, door QR, Google or phone sign-in): ticks as today; the server now keeps which
  terms version, when, and how (join, sign-in, re-ask).
- Member at joining: trainer sharing and entry-machine sharing are tick boxes, off until ticked (HIM question 2).
- Member: Settings → About shows "Privacy notice" and "Terms" rows that open the pages.
- Desk adding a member: a birthday that shows under 18 asks for HIM question 1's rule.
- Desk: a member profile has a "No offers" switch; offer and assistant lists leave those members out.
- Gym owner: asked once to accept the 14 Nov 2026 terms. HQ: each gym's accepted version and date are visible.
- Must NOT see:
  - an under-18 member told their workouts are "saved automatically";
  - any server data of a member whose age is under 18, after the app has seen that age;
  - the tick box again when it was already ticked for the current version (on any phone of the same account);
  - a member with "No offers" on any offer list or assistant WhatsApp batch;
  - a member over 18 blocked because of an old age number (turning 18 must unblock).

## 4. Questions
[F] questions with evidence:
- Where is the age? Phone SQLite `user_profile.age INTEGER NOT NULL` (apps/mobile/src/db/schema.ts). The rule is
  `backupAllowedForAge` (apps/mobile/src/cloud/phoneAccount.ts: under 18 blocked, unknown or 0 not blocked).
- Where is the stop? `profileUnder18()` (apps/mobile/src/cloud/copySync.ts) skips uploads; `accountStore.ts`
  refuses phone sign-in for under 18. Neither deletes.
- Can the server copy be deleted? Yes: `member_account_delete()` (supabase 0032) deletes `member_items`, and
  deletes the whole account when the person has no gym. For under-18 a NARROWER call is needed that deletes only
  the copy (`member_items`, `member_summary`) and keeps the sign-in (new RPC `member_copy_clear`).
- Where is the tick? `apps/mobile/src/shop/joinTerms.ts`: `TERMS_AGREED_VERSION = '2026-11-10'`, stored in the
  phone's `meta` table under `terms_agreed`. Not sent to the server.
- Server join functions: `member_join` (latest 0020), `self_join_send` (latest 0089), phone sign-in (0099). None
  takes a terms version or an adult tick.
- Gym terms: `gym_terms_acceptance` and `_terms_versions()` (0033; its header says NOT APPLIED, but 0066 refers to
  the table and 0067 says APPLIED LIVE). UNKNOWN whether 0033 is live: checked with a plain SQL read before build.
- Watch: does the watch read the age? UNKNOWN: searched during build (file boundary is updated first if so).
(HIM) questions:
1. Desk adds a person under 18. My view: allow it only with a "A parent or guardian agreed" tick, record who
   ticked and when, keep that member out of every MettleUp cloud feature, and change privacy notice section 9 to
   say gyms may keep their own records of members under 18. Indian gyms do have 15 to 17 year-old members, so a
   hard block costs gyms. Option B: block under 18 at the desk. A lawyer should confirm either way.
2. Trainer and entry-machine sharing at joining. My view: make them tick boxes, off by default. Option B: keep
   them on and ask a lawyer first.
3. The new words (under-18 line, re-ask screen, privacy notice section 9 change, "No offers") need his yes before
   they ship.

## 7. Copies of the data
- Birth year: phone only (user_profile). Erased by "Erase all data". Not backed up for under 18.
- Consent record (new server table `member_consent`: account, terms version, adult yes, at, how, gym if any):
  append-only; deleted when the account is deleted (member_account_delete must include it: R-632 adds it to the
  one list); kept as proof while the account exists.
- Under-18 delete: removes `member_items` and `member_summary` for the account; the phone's local data stays.
- "No offers" mark: the gym's member row; deleted with the member.
- Gym terms acceptance: existing table; unchanged.

## 8. Risks and the pre-mortem
It failed one month after launch because:
- Age converted wrongly on upgrade (a 0 or null age blocked someone): guard = unknown age stays "not blocked";
  tests for 0, null, 17, 18 and a birthday crossing.
- The under-18 delete ran for an adult (typo 1 for 21, then fixed): guard = a clear confirm sheet before the
  delete ("This deletes your saved copy on MettleUp's server"); the delete runs only after that tap.
- The re-ask blocked gym features for members who never open the app: guard = the gym's desk still works; only
  data sent from the phone waits.
- The server refused old app versions that send no terms version: guard = the new parameters are optional; an
  old app's join still works and is recorded as "version unknown".
- iPhone build broke: guard = no Android-only code; the iPhone check list runs.
