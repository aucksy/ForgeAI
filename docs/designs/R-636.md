# R-636: The watch keeps the workout on screen and shows heart rate while dim

Lane: Full (a background sensor, a screen kept on by the app, battery, a store-shipped watch app). Card: docs/changes/R-636.json.
Family (the cause): the watch app treats "nobody touched the WATCH" as "nobody is training", and treats "the screen is dim"
as "nobody needs the heart rate".
Round 2 (28 Nov 2026): rewritten after three fresh reviews (newcomer 14 findings, critic 17, promises 10). Every finding is
answered in section 10.

## Words used in this note
- **Watch app**: the MettleUp app on a Wear OS watch (folder apps/mobile/android/wear). The phone app sends it the open
  workout; the watch shows it and can log sets.
- **Always-on display (AOD)**: a watch setting. ON = the watch itself dims the screen when the wrist goes down (the system's
  "ambient" mode). OFF = the screen would go black; so the app keeps the screen on and dims it itself (**self-dim**, built in
  change R-442, file SelfDim.kt).
- **Held**: the app keeps the screen on (flag FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON). **Let go**: the app clears that flag; the watch's own
  screen timeout then turns the screen black and, on the next wake, the watch face shows, not MettleUp.
- **Idle wait**: 12 minutes (SelfDim.IDLE_RELEASE_MS). The cloud test shortens it with a test-only key (SelfDim.QA_IDLE_KEY).
- **Watch diary**: AmbientDiag, a short log of screen events (on, off, dim, held, let go, with a reason word). A member can
  send it to us from the phone app (apps/mobile/src/lib/watchDiag.ts keeps only known reason words, list SELF_DIM_WHY).
- **Owner observations O-05 (27 Nov) and O-40 (28 Nov)**: the owner's own reports of this problem (docs/OWNER-OBSERVATIONS.md).
  **Backlog rows F243** (watch heart rate; "step A" = this change, "step B" = Wear OS exercise mode later) and **F244** (the
  workout leaves the watch screen by itself) are in docs/OWNER-INBOX.md.
- **Gates G0-G7**: the "No gaps" review steps (skill change-360). **M1-M19**: this project's extra questions
  (docs/change-360-project.md).

## 1. The problem, in the user's words
The owner (Aakash), 27 Nov 2026 (O-05) and again 28 Nov 2026 (O-40): "the watch app not capturing the heart rate when it's
dim, and it also is automatically going in the background for some reason which also turns off the screen because it's not in
the foreground. It does show that semi-circular pill at the bottom of the watch indicating a workout is active, but if it goes
in the background automatically it basically turns off the screen and probably stops capturing the heart rate too."
He keeps Always-on display OFF on purpose, so self-dim is what he sees. He logs most sets on the PHONE.

How the watch app works today (phone app 0.80.0 with its watch app):
- While a workout is open and AOD is off, the app holds the screen and dims it itself after the watch's screen timeout.
- It lets go in two cases (SelfDim.releaseWhy): reason **"idle"** = 12 minutes with no tap, crown, button or wrist raise ON
  THE WATCH (phone logging does not count); reason **"paused"** = at once when the phone's workout timer is paused. After a
  let-go the screen goes black and the next wake shows the watch face, with only the system's "workout active" pill (from the
  app's foreground WorkoutService).
- Heart rate (HeartRate.kt, Health Services MeasureClient) is read only while the screen is up AND NOT dim
  (MainActivity: `measure = onScreen && !dimmed && hrAllowed && (workoutActive || rest != null)`), a battery choice from the
  0.60.0 review. Dim screens also hide the heart line in two places: WatchLayout.heartLine (older rest screens) and the
  logging screens' TopBar (LogScreens.kt `if (heart != null && !ambient)`). During a workout the logging screens are what
  show, because the phone always marks the workout as "can log on the watch" (WearSync.kt).
- Heart rate is ONLY SHOWN. It is never saved, sent to the phone, written in the diary or kept in a workout.

Why "it goes to the background by itself": a member logging on the phone does not touch the watch for 12 minutes, and every
pause on the phone lets go at once. Both are normal in a gym. Accepted risk: his diary from those workouts was not read
(it reaches us only when he sends it). The code path explains both symptoms; his gym check after release (section 9) and the
diary reason words confirm it.

## 5. The rules (exact)
- **Activity time** = max(inputAt, changeAt). inputAt stays as today (watch input only) and alone drives the watch's own
  bright-to-dim timeout (5-30 s) and the wrist-down quiet window. changeAt is used ONLY by the idle wait. While the sensor
  says OFF_WRIST, changeAt is not moved (only watch input counts).
- **A workout change** = the screen's copy of the workout (WatchState.Snapshot) changes in any field except `accent` (the
  gym colour): rest, workoutActive, title, text (set line; covers pause and resume), next, overAt, savedAt, logFlow, entry,
  row. It does not matter whether the phone or the watch caused it (a rest started, ±15 or Skip on the watch, the watch's
  own rest alarm): each is real use of the workout. An equal snapshot (retry, flush, re-send) is not a change. The first
  snapshot after the app starts is the baseline, not a change. Pure function `SelfDim.workoutChanged(old, new)`.
- **releaseWhy(now, activityAt, idleMs)**: "idle" when now - activityAt >= idleMs, else null. The instant "paused" reason is
  removed. The reason word stays "idle" for a paused workout too, so the tap re-hold (`released == "idle"`) keeps working
  and no "paused" word is needed. The resume branch (`released == "paused"`) is deleted with it.
- **After a let-go**: a workout change while MettleUp is still on screen re-holds with diary reason "change". It does NOT
  reset inputAt (today's hold path sets inputAt = now at MainActivity:599; for a change-made hold it sets changeAt instead).
  If the screen is already black, nothing happens until a wrist raise or tap (as today after "idle").
- **measureHeart(onScreen, systemDim, selfDim, held, allowed, workoutOrRest, locked)** (pure, in SelfDim):
  true when onScreen && allowed && workoutOrRest && !locked && !systemDim && (!selfDim || held). So: bright screen as today;
  self-dim only while held; never in the system's own dim (AOD on); never on the "needs MettleUp Plus" screen.
- **Heart line when dim** (WatchCopy.heartLine in WatchLayout.kt, used by WatchScreens.kt, and LogScreens TopBar): it gets
  the sensor status; when dim it is shown only when status == READING and bpm > 0; never "—". Bright: as today.
- **Diary**: two new reason words, "change" (held again after a workout change) and "off-wrist" (once, when the sensor
  says off the wrist during a hold). Let-go stays "self-dim-off idle". Both words are added to the phone's list SELF_DIM_WHY
  (watchDiag.ts) so they are not dropped; they say what the screen did, never what the workout holds. The phone summary
  stops counting "paused" let-goes and its legend becomes "idle = no change on phone or watch for 12 min". Never a heart
  number (a test asserts it).

## 8. Risks and the pre-mortem
- A forgotten workout keeps the watch dim-on and the sensor running: bounded by the 12-minute idle wait (re-sends do not count)
  and the 4-hour open-workout limit.
- Rest timers ending by themselves: a rest end is a change once; at worst one more 12-minute wait after the last rest.
- Battery: sensor on while self-dimmed for the whole workout. Not measured. This reverses the 0.60.0 battery choice for
  self-dim only, at the owner's request. His gym check records battery % at start and end; if drain is bad, step B (exercise
  mode, batched readings) is the fix.
- Redraws: the dim screen redraws on each reading (about once a second) while self-dimmed. The screen is already on and the
  dim rest countdown already redraws every second, so no new wake-up of the processor. But outside rests the dim screen used to redraw once a minute; now it redraws on
  each reading for the whole held workout. Accepted; step B (exercise mode, batched readings) lowers it.
- Layout jump: the dim text block re-centres once when the first reading arrives. Usually the sensor is already reading from
  the bright screen, so the dim screen starts with the line. Accepted.
- The emulator passes but a real Pixel Watch still lets go: his gym check, and the diary reason tells us why.
