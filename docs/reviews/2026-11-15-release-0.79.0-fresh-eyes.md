  error and the trainers-not-added line have only unit tests (owner rule: UAT every change before release).

## 2. Behaviour bugs (concrete cases)

**G1. A found member who joins a gym with its own colours, then hits a setup save failure, is dropped on the
start screen with no message.** `goStraightIn` joins the gym. The gym's colours bump `useGymTheme.epoch`, and
`RootFrame key={epoch}` (app/_layout.tsx) rebuilds the welcome. If `useOnboarding.complete()` then throws (or the
draft is refused), the answer is `'steps'`. `WelcomePhone.enter` calls `onSteps` on the old, unmounted screen, so
the 3 questions never open. The new screen shows "Continue with your phone number" again, the gym is already
joined, and the typed name is gone. One case: a phone that kept its own workouts (`ExistingDataError` path) and a
branded gym. Fix: keep "needs the 3 questions" in the `useStraightIn` store (outside the screen), the same way
"Opening <gym>..." is kept. Not run; read from code.

**G2. A member on a new phone who uses the phone button gets an empty app; the backup is never offered.**
Before 0.79.0 the big button was "Continue with Google", which finds the Drive backup or server copy. Now
straight-in finishes setup at once. Nothing is lost (a later Google sign-in merges the server copy), but the
member sees no history, Previous columns are empty, and targets start from scratch, with no hint that "Try another
way -> Continue with Google" brings it back.

**G3. The welcome's watch screen may miss real watches.** The intro waits 1.5 s for the watch answer
(`INTRO_WATCH_WAIT_MS`). R-575 changed `askWatches` to wait for all 6 Wear OS answers (up to 4.5 s, with retries).
On a freshly installed app the Wear OS client is cold, so any slow step means 2 screens and no watch screen. The
notes say the watch screen was only ever seen through the QA stand-in.

**G4. The watch update pop-up can show for a watch that is out of reach.** A watch with a diary newer than 7
days but not connected now (`connected=false`) still raises "Your watch app has an update". The only answer
"Update on watch" can give is "Your watch is not connected". It is better to wait until the watch is connected
(the 20 s / 2 min retries already exist).

**G5 (minor). Privacy line vs. what is kept.** "Your number is checked once and is not kept for this." A
straight-in member's number is saved into Profile (`straightInDraft` -> `user_profile.phone`) without being asked.
It is local only, but the member never typed it into Profile. Either say so, or leave Profile's number empty.

## 4. Improvements and gaps (first-time member on a phone), ranked

1. **Most new members have no MettleUp gym, and the only button sends them through WhatsApp to "not found".**
   Screen 1 shows just "Continue with your phone number"; "Use without an account" sits under "Try another way".
   A Play install with a non-MettleUp gym has to send a WhatsApp message and wait before learning there is
   nothing to find. Hevy and Strong get a lifter logging in 1-2 taps. Suggestion: on the gym intro screen, which
   already asks "Does your gym use MettleUp?", offer two answers. "Yes" goes to the phone form; "No / not sure"
   goes to setup. This is the owner's product call, since he asked for phone-only.
2. **The phone path should offer a returning member's workouts** (G2): after straight-in or "not found", show
   "Used MettleUp before? Bring back your workouts" (Google), so the targets keep the history.
3. **One extra screen.** "Get started" on the last intro screen opens a second hero ("Get stronger every week")
   with one button. Go straight to the phone form, or put the button on the intro. It saves a tap and a repeated
   headline.
4. **The sign-in boxes use placeholders as their only labels.** At his text size, "Your first name, as your gym
   has it" is cut to "...as your gym" (picture tour-10, run 43737450694), and both labels disappear once typing
   starts. Put a short label above each box.
5. **Straight-in sets "Build muscle", "New to lifting" and kg without saying so.** An experienced lifter is set up
   as a beginner and never told. Show one line on the first Workout screen: "We set you up as New to lifting,
   Build muscle. Change in Profile."
6. **The message picker starts on "Gym owner" for every type.** A member asking a workout question, who has their
   own trainer, reaches only the owner unless they change the chip. Start a Question on "<Name> (your trainer)"
   when the gym set one. This is a product call: say it to the owner.
7. The intro screens cannot be swiped (buttons only). Every top app swipes.
8. Picture tour-02 (gym intro, 380aacf8, text 1.3) shows the gym card cut at the bottom. 346a6c47 (shrink floor
   0.35) should fix it, but its picture run was still going: look at it before the tag.
9. The welcome's first screen still says "Beat your last workout", "TODAY'S TARGET" and "a little above last time"
   (owner rule: Previous / Next). The title is his approved exception ("keep last"); the column header and body
   are not covered by that exception. Ask him once.
