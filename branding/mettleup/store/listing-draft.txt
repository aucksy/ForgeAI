TITLE: MettleUp: Gym Workout Tracker
SHORT: Log every set, beat last week's weight, and see your strength grow. Free.
FULL1: MettleUp is a free workout log for the gym. Tap each set as you finish it, and see what you lifted last time right beside it.
FULL2: Beat last week: each exercise shows the kilos to beat today, and the app tells you the moment you set a new record.
FULL3: See your progress: a chart for every lift, a body map of the muscles you trained, body weight and private progress photos.
FULL4: Plans that work: ready programs like Push Pull Legs, or build your own routines in folders.
FULL5: Works without internet: no signal in the gym, no problem. Your workouts stay on your phone.
FULL6: Better with your gym: if your gym uses MettleUp, join with its code or QR poster. You get your trainer's plans, check in with one scan, see your membership, and order from the gym's shop.
FULL7: Bring your history: import your workouts from Hevy or Strong.
FULL8: Free to use. No ads.
