TITLE: MettleUp: Gym Workout Tracker
SHORT: Gym log and workout planner: beat last time's weight on every set, even offline.
FULL1: MettleUp is a gym workout tracker and workout log for weight lifting and strength training. Use it as your gym log: tap each set as you finish it, and see what you lifted last time right beside it.
FULL2: LOG EVERY SET
FULL3: • Log weight and reps, time or distance for more than 400 exercises with steps and drawings, or add your own.
FULL4: • Mark warm-up, drop and failure sets, and rate how hard each set felt (RPE).
FULL5: • Pair exercises into supersets and keep a note on any exercise.
FULL6: • A rest timer starts after every set and buzzes when rest is over, even with the screen off.
FULL7: • Tick off a set from the live workout card in your phone's notifications, without opening the app.
FULL8: • The plate calculator shows which plates to load, using the plates and bars your gym really has.
FULL9: BEAT LAST TIME (PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD)
FULL10: • Each exercise suggests today's weight and reps from your own history, so you add a little weight or one more rep at a time.
FULL11: • The start screen shows the kilos to beat today.
FULL12: • After your workout, see which exercises went up, stayed the same or went down since last time.
FULL13: • A pop-up tells you the moment you set a new record: heaviest weight, most reps or best set.
FULL14: WORKOUT PLANNER AND ROUTINES
FULL15: • Start a ready program: Push Pull Legs (PPL), Upper / Lower, Full Body Basics or Strength Starter.
FULL16: • Or let the app build a plan for what you have: a full gym, dumbbells, a bar, or no equipment for a home workout.
FULL17: • Build your own routines and keep them in folders.
FULL18: • Take an easy week every 6 weeks if you want one, without spoiling your records.
FULL19: SEE YOUR PROGRESS
FULL20: • A chart for every lift, plus weekly charts of kilos lifted, reps and time.
FULL21: • A body map shows which muscles you trained in the last 7 days.
FULL22: • See your strength level (Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced) for squat, bench press, deadlift and other main lifts, and work out your 1-rep max.
FULL23: • Track body weight and measurements, and keep private progress photos to compare side by side.
FULL24: • Get a monthly report, a year in review and badges as you keep training.
FULL25: YOUR WORKOUTS ARE SAFE
FULL26: • Works offline: log a full workout with no internet in the gym.
FULL27: • Back up to Google Drive, or save one backup file you can keep.
FULL28: • Export your workouts to Excel.
FULL29: • Switching apps? Import your workout history and routines from Hevy or Strong.
FULL30: • Use kg or pounds, and send your workouts to Health Connect.
FULL31: WATCH AND PHONE EXTRAS
FULL32: • Wear OS watch app: see the rest countdown, feel a buzz when rest ends, check your heart rate and log sets from your wrist.
FULL33: • Home-screen widgets, and workout reminders on the days and times you pick.
FULL34: BETTER WITH YOUR GYM
FULL35: • If your gym uses MettleUp, join with its code or QR poster.
FULL36: • Check in with one scan, and see your plan, end date and days left.
FULL37: • Get your gym's messages and offers, its opening hours, and how busy it is right now.
FULL38: • Ask the desk to pause or renew your plan from the app.
FULL39: • If your gym offers them, you also get your trainer's workout and diet plans, the gym's shop, and a monthly challenge with a leaderboard.
FULL40: Logging your workouts is free, and there are no ads. Download MettleUp and start your first workout today.
