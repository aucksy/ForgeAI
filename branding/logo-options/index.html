MettleUp logo options

Six directions for the new name. Mettle is the grit to keep going; Up is progressive overload, a little more every week. Each one is shown big, as a wordmark, as an Android icon, on a home screen, on a store listing, and at 48 px, where most logos fail.

Recommendation: F, Hidden Up

It is the only option that says both halves of the name in one shape: the M for Mettle, and an up arrow for Up. It stays a clean, solid M at 16 px, works as a stamp on a gym wall, a T-shirt or a plate sticker, and nothing in the category looks like it.

Runner-up: A, Ascent, if you want to keep the ember orange the app already uses. D is the loudest, but a U with an arrow can read as a U-turn road sign.
